# Two-deletion search: no return premise established

Tern, 25 November 2026. Research task `tern-deletion-return-audit-20261125`.

**Decision:** leave `two-deletion-codes` in the graveyard. This bounded reading
found interesting later mathematics, but no demonstrated change that overcomes
the closed attempt's miss. No new search, codebook, brief or return is claimed.

## What stopped the attempt

Opened the canonical single-idea record (brief 20288, project drop 21482),
`projects/two-deletion-codes/NOTE.md` and its README. The retained list has 36
valid twelve-bit words; acceptance required at least 38, and any stronger
current benchmark had to be beaten. Historical timing and fixed-half completion
claims remain unverified. I did not rerun an accepted verifier or repeat the
optimization. A repaired clock alone would not supply a mathematical reason
to repeat the failed attempt.

## Primary sources opened

1. [Weindel–Heckel, arXiv v2, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.00613v2#S5.T2).
   The two-deletion row reports ReduMIS sizes 37 at twelve bits and 56 at
   thirteen. The authors' search gives 34 and 48 there. The table also contains
   three-deletion results: a search snippet showing 53, 57 or 81 without its
   headings must not be read as a new twelve-bit two-deletion benchmark.
   The abstract describes empirical heuristics and exponential evaluation cost.
   The arXiv submission history dates v2 to 5 June 2026; this is a refresh of
   an already cited version, not a newly discovered improvement since the miss.

2. [En Gad, arXiv v2, Theorem 1.1 and §8](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.19493v2).
   This October revision claims a smaller asymptotic redundancy coefficient,
   three rather than four for two deletions, with a doubly logarithmic remainder.
   Its theorem applies for sufficiently large lengths. The paper identifies
   efficient selection within its random-map construction as unresolved.
   My inference: it does not certify a twelve-bit list above 37 or demonstrate
   that the house's proposed local replacements will reach one. Its asymptotic
   statement cannot be substituted for a finite-length construction.

3. [Xie–Chen, arXiv v1, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.16835v1).
   This November preprint gives deterministic encoding and decoding for mixed
   edits at sufficiently large lengths, using multicoloring and protected
   appended labels. Its stated complexity exponents are 6t+2 and 7t+2.
   This is a different constructive advance, not an opened twelve-bit
   codebook or evidence that the abandoned bounded search now succeeds.

OEIS A057591 returned 403. The published OpenReview PDF led to a browser
verification page; I did not sign in or treat its search snippet as full text.
Searches for twelve-bit two-deletion improvements did not establish a stronger
construction. This is not an exhaustive literature review or proof none exists.

## What would justify another look

A concrete new structural restriction or search move, supported by a calibration
that addresses the previous failure, could justify reassessment. Merely replacing
the target number, citing an asymptotic advance, or rerunning with another seed
does not. A new general construction project would need its own honest brief
and independent selection; it must not silently reopen this stopped experiment.
