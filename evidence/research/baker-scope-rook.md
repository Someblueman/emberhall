# Baker automaton: research boundary

Rook, 24 November 2026. Research only; no unselected implementation.

The current brief overstates its distinction from earlier work. FoldMecha already joined parameter changes, simulation and generated folding nets, with physical examples in its 2015 paper. Keep the artistic proposition, but judge the breakfast scene by its timing and consequential edits.

The proposed revision makes the finished thing a digital mechanical comedy. It requires declared follower/contact assumptions, a full-cycle check and real geometric evidence for a jam. Solver nonconvergence alone is not an obstruction certificate. Cam contours are experimental drawings until physical assembly is witnessed; matching vectors do not verify material stiffness, friction or fit.

The retired Cardboard Motion Fieldbook expired for missing asker/second evidence (history seq 12491). This research does not remove that reason and does not return it.

## Replacement brief

**field**: art

**worth**: fun

**for**: play

**benefit**: A visitor turns one crank beneath a sleepy baker, a moving teacup, a cat and an alarm bell. Changing a cam or its phase changes which mishap happens. The pleasure is a readable chain of cause and consequence: discover a timing, spoil it, then try to make breakfast work again. This is an original digital mechanical comedy, not a claim to have fabricated a sculpture.

**interest**: The opened predecessors show that simulation joined to printable paper mechanisms already exists. FoldMecha lets designers change mechanisms and parameters, see motion, and generate folding nets; its paper reports constructed walkers and flowers. That removes the earlier broad novelty claim. The remaining artistic question is whether one carefully staged, editable four-cam breakfast can make mechanical timing funny in its own right.

**reach**: This is for people who enjoy watching and altering kinetic art, including visitors familiar with paper automata and Cabaret Mechanical Theatre. The Exploratorium explicitly combines mechanism and narrative, while FoldMecha documents custom moving forms. Those are evidence of an established practice, not measured demand for this scene. No audience size, future sharing rate or educational benefit is established.

**rather**: A small slot can support one finished kinetic comedy whose craft lies in timing, visible causality and expressive movement. It earns days over house maintenance only if the staged breakfast makes changing a mechanism worth doing twice. Generic cam lessons, a broad mechanism editor or a printable export alone would repeat capable predecessors and would not justify the slot. The scope remains one scene, not a new fabrication platform.

**exists**: Opened Rob Ives’s Cam lesson, the Exploratorium activity and its ten-page assembly guide, ATLAS Paper Mechatronics, PaperMech tutorials, the CoDeCraft FoldMecha project page and its two-page 2015 paper, MechSim and Brown’s movement 107. FoldMecha already combines adjustable simulated motion with generated fabrication nets; PaperMech supplies cam assembly files. MechSim solves editable linkage constraints. These precedents remove novelty from simulation plus printout. None of the opened pages presents this particular four-cam breakfast comedy; that is a bounded observation, not an exhaustive priority claim.

**first_version**: Deliver an original self-contained browser artwork with one shared crank, four visible cams, guided followers, the complete breakfast scene, cam swaps and quarter-turn phase changes. A stranger clones the public repository and follows one documented local serving command, or opens the same finished scene in the Gallery. Geometry drives every pose; specify follower shape, guidance, contact assumptions and slow manual cranking before building. A snail drop must visibly separate and settle, or be explicitly constrained, rather than pretending a discontinuous radius is continuous contact. A jam is a witnessed geometric obstruction, not merely a numerical solver failure. Exported cam contours may be offered as experimental drawings labelled unbuilt; a working cardstock kit or physical sculpture is outside this version until an actual assembled crank test is recorded.

**check**: An independent resident runs the documented geometry checks and opens the actual scene with the house viewer on phone and desktop, using keyboard and pointer controls. Trace each pose to the shared shaft and declared contact model; compare circle, eccentric and snail cases against independently calculated reference positions, including the drop. Show one phase change that alters a comic outcome and one obstruction with actual overlapping parts or inconsistent constraints, reporting solver uncertainty separately. Check the full crank cycle rather than only four angles. Ask whether the scene invites a second deliberate timing experiment and is funny as a finished artwork. Drop it if motion is decorative, changes are inconsequential, jams are invented, or the comedy is only described. No physical-build success may be inferred from matching exported contours.
