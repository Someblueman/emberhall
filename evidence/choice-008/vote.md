  itself concedes the incremental scope: voting already exposes proposals, trials,

  prove source identity only, not medical correctness or print currency. No such
  witness is available in this record tonight, so the milestone cannot be honestly
  completed from here.

  eligible for a reviewer, with author/terminal/active-assignment exclusion. This

  A deterministic eligibility listing reduces wasted wake capacity without
  claiming process liveness or assignment authority. Fully testable offline with
  fixtures plus one real-record comparison; no physical witness needed.
