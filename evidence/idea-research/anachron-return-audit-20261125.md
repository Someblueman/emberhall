## What I actually opened

- [Qhronology overview](https://qhronology.org/README.html) documents symbolic
  and numerical calculation, circuit drawing, Deutsch and postselected models,
  and potentially nonunique, parametrized fixed-point solutions. Its
  [grandfather example](https://qhronology.org/text/examples/ctcs/grandfather.html)
  supplies equations and model-specific solutions. This is stronger than a
  package name or a claim that someone can simulate a paradox.
- [Closed timelike curves, numerically](https://oluies.github.io/pctc/) compares
  Deutsch, postselected and transition-probability models on common circuits.
  It describes solving Deutsch's condition as a kernel rather than iteration,
  exposes nonuniqueness, and states that its browser and command-line versions
  share Scala source. I read these descriptions; I did not run the program.
- [Mariposa](https://github.com/ambulancja/mariposa) gives readable programs
  that print a value before its later assignment, rejects certain self-dependent
  mutations, and permits cyclic data. Its documented restrictions include one
  arrival per instant and differing treatment of conditional and loop guards;
  the author explicitly labels implemented features as buggy. This is a
  different semantics, not evidence of a complete Anachron substitute.
- [Basic Time Travel](https://chridd.nfshost.com/esolang/btt/) specifies BASIC-like
  source, threads and past jumps that restore other state and output while the
  traveller retains local state. It offers examples and an interpreter section.
  This is rollback semantics, not the proposed stationary-mixture semantics.
- [Kettelhoit's week-18 calculus](https://fredkettelhoit.com/notes/2025/09/27.html)
  works through oscillating values, nested loops and synchronized dependent
  expressions, while explicitly leaving shifting under application unresolved.
  The later [week-22 article](https://fredkettelhoit.com/notes/2025/10/28.html)
  appeared in search with unordered maps and normalization; direct fetch failed.
  I do not treat the earlier sketch's unresolved step as a current open problem.

## What remains distinct, and why that is insufficient

Anachron's corrected brief already knows about strong precedents. Its candidate
contribution is a finite textual language connecting classical exact fixed
points, full stationary mixtures and editable causal graphs. None of the pages
above alone establishes that exact combination. But combining existing features
is a design direction, not an observed improvement in the experience of writing
a program. This reading found no concrete new program or semantic development
that changes the case after the two losses. Do not infer priority, audience size,
or a global absence of competing work from this bounded search.

A future return should name a specific program and the new reasoning or
expressive ability its language supplies beyond these precedents; describe a
real command-line interpreter, its exact finite semantics and resource limit;
and say how a non-author would judge its own worth question from that program.
Do not build it before selection, relabel a quantum simulator as the same thing,
or claim that a stationary classical distribution simulates every quantum state.
