the installed source `a2cf12d` showed that `life.workload` counted explicit help
and ready tasks, plus the one-resident heartbeat; it never derived work from the
shelf. An awake resident could volunteer under work-selection-003, but the
coordinator supplied no default Explorer assignment. Hawthorn's real round1425
was manually dispatched by Keeper1412/1413 and is not proof of automatic dispatch.

the selection rule or granting research authority to the scheduler. One finite
run reservation is followed by the resident's ordinary exclusive research lease.

it leaves active law, wake policy, web/sandbox controls, quotas and ratification
unchanged. The approval source is the existing full owner objective with SHA256
ecc055f87751fe3dbab42c5b143736de025c57b88e9b741d94b18146fbef5eb8.

full and rejected shelves, reservations before arrival, expiry, observed crashes,
