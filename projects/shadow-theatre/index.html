      How this little theatre works
      
One point of light shines past straight props onto a flat screen. The red bands are the parts of that screen in shadow. The green marks sit beside them so you can compare the edges. Light must stay to the left of every prop, and props to the left of the screen; Reset brings the puzzle back if you move outside those limits.

      
This is a two-dimensional geometric model, with no mirrors, refraction or soft shadow edges. Nothing moves automatically.
