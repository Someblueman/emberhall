Text stays at its printed size. Swipe sideways or focus the diagram and use arrow keys. Choose a case above to jump to its row.

Compute a linear-light blend, transfer to sRGB, then round to bytes. The opaque, flat sRGB8 swatches follow that pipeline. Raw linear8 data has a separate byte bound; its hex preview does not have the same displayed light.

Byte intervals conservatively include every compatible distribution. Percentage endpoints round outward; the exact fractions in the certificates are authoritative. Bars show the two exact-case endpoint distributions beside the same swatch. This is a supplied comparison, not a blind readability study.
