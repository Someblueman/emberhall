22 November 2026. The comparison series is stopped, with **zero further

## What remains usable

The accepted M1/M2 work is a finite-palette probability model, an importable
renderer, exact and conservative byte-observation certificates, and a
[ten-case vector atlas](legends/first_legend.html). Open its
[SVG](legends/first_legend.svg), inspect the [certificates](legends/witnesses.json),
or follow the [regeneration and import instructions](README.md).
The [M2 review](REVIEW_M2.md) records mathematical, reproducibility and repaired
phone/desktop visual acceptance; the README records the M1 acceptance.

This is a real, reproducible mathematical instrument: another mind can check
what event probabilities a blended colour permits. That is its demonstrated
worth. It has not established better reader performance than explicit
distributions. The [model](MODEL.md) assumes a known finite palette and nominal
rational linear-light sRGB; [byte bounds](DISPLAY.md) use conservative closed
outer boxes. Calibrated display output and reader benefit across palettes or
human populations are outside these results.

## What the two comparisons found

| Frozen report | Distribution / atlas scores | Distribution / atlas mean clarity | Execution outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [First comparison](COMPARISON_RESULT.md) | 20/20 / 20/20 | 3.4 / 4.0 | INCONCLUSIVE |
| [Second comparison](COMPARISON_V2_RESULT.md) | 20/20 / 20/20 | 3.6 / 4.6 | INCONCLUSIVE |

The first clarity gain was 0.6, below the required 1. The second gain is
exactly `(23 - 18) / 5 = 1`, but the frozen scorer subtracts binary floating-point
means: `4.6 - 3.6 = 0.9999999999999996`, below its `>= 1.0` gate. This is a
documented arithmetic boundary defect, not an exact clarity shortfall. The
diagnosis does not turn the preserved execution into a pass. Neither responses,
scorers, thresholds nor frozen packs were changed for this closeout.

Both were non-author model-reader comparisons with different matched case
sets, a fixed presentation order and perfect objective scores. They do not
isolate presentation effects from case difficulty or establish human benefit.
The [prospective decision](PROSPECTIVE_DECISION.md) explains why another reader
under this design would not resolve those limits. There is no third attempt,
repaired rescore, replacement reader, new pack or reader recruitment in this
series. Any future research would require a separately authorized question
and design.
