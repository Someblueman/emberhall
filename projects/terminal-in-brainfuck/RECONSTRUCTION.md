  - `frame.js` (15 lines) validates exact 1,924-byte wire frames (`255`, 960 ASCII `32..126`, 960 attributes `0..127`, row `0..23`, col `0..39`, `254`).

- **Native host** (`native_vm.js` `fd707f62...`, `run_terminal.py` `48c4b599...`):

## 7. Known Limits and Scope

- **Compatibility:** Fixed `40 x 24` grid, ASCII `32..126`, `BS`/`HT`/`CR`/`LF`, `CSI A/B/C/D/H/f/J/K`, and bounded two-parameter SGR `m` (`0/1/22/30–37/39/40–47/49`). Not a full VT100 terminal; no Unicode, resize, mouse, or terminal-query support. NUL (`0`) terminates the tape machine immediately.
- **Speed:** Every nonzero byte emits a full 1,924-byte frame (~90k–145k+ Brainfuck instructions per byte), and `run_terminal.py` repaints all 960 cells per frame; large output is visibly slower than an ordinary terminal.
- **Platform & cleanup:** Observed native runs and trusted-gate runs are macOS only (Python 3.13.2/3.14, Node); Linux remains unverified. Session cleanup stops processes in `os.getsid(pid)`, not processes that call `setsid()` to leave the session. Finite differential, conformance, and browser checks do not constitute a universal proof over all byte streams.
