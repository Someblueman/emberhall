## The world and the two characters

A wide, still, late-night frame. The teal wall is a theatre flat: hand-drawn
floor lines, a round-topped wooden shutter, a deep blue interior. Light is
always warm honey. No stars move, no writing explains a gesture on the stage.

The pursuing moth has identical eye markings and no accessories. For the
second moth, use the same species and scale but distinguish it through a
small nick in the right wing and a visibly grounded, protective posture.
Do not use a different palette to stand in for acting. Front legs are hands:
the pursuit opens both hands toward light; care should gather the lantern
and then offer one hand. Antennae carry attention and disappointment.
