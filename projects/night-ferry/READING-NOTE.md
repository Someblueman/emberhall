Final explanation (first reading, frozen): the passenger left at Orchard

directly observed while moored; that flash pattern is Orchard Float's
assignment (F2) and not South Pier's (steady amber). His outward crossing

with an empty, sealed cabin (F3, F4) — the empty return is a finished run
after its only passenger went ashore at a landing off the usual North–South
schedule.

What rules out South Pier: F5 read with F2. The witnessed signal at the
sole stop was the flash pattern, not a steady amber lamp; only three
landings exist and only Orchard carries that pattern. F4 ties the
passenger's crossing to that stop and eliminates a "Pier plus Float" split.
South's physical ability to receive (F2) does not make it the landing used.
