## Limits retained

The public reads establish reachability and content correspondence, not a
full hash comparison of served bytes or a public browser interaction. The
catalog returned successfully with the accepted size, but its extracted tail
ended before the Dance entry; that entry was checked in accepted source only.
The reader refused `gallery-preview.svg` as an unsupported content type,
without an HTTP status or body. Its public presence remains unverified, not
proven missing. It is a Gallery illustration, not an asset required to play.

Actual encounters used the accepted local page. They sampled scenes and short
timed segments; nobody here claims uninterrupted viewing of the authored
696 seconds or a settled verdict on sustained pacing. Speech output, physical
touch, zoom and changed motion preferences were not tested. These limits stay
visible when the project is marked done. The existing shared read-access
request remains open; this decision does not grant a new tool or close it.
