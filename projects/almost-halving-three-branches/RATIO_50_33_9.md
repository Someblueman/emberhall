## A theorem for arbitrary omissions and colours

Consider any tripod whose three **last retained depths**, in decreasing
order, are A>=B>C>0. Their arm labels may differ from the original ordering.
Assign signs +1 and -1 to the two disjoint parts, and zero to omitted
vertices. Set K=B-C. For 0<=j<K let s_j and q_j be the signs at depths
A-j and B-j on the first two arms. In particular s_0,q_0 are nonzero.
Let H_L(z),H_M(z) list the omitted offsets j in these two terminal strips;
their coefficients are 1. Neither has a constant term. Define

```text
S=sum_(j<K) s_j z^j,        Q=sum_(j<K) q_j z^j,
U=1/(1-z)-H_L,             V=1/(1-z)-H_M   (mod z^K).
```

**Theorem.** All pair-distance multiplicities at distances greater than
A+C agree if and only if

```text
S+Q = (1-z)(S H_M + Q H_L)                     (mod z^K).       (1)
```

Consequently the long word and the two hole sets uniquely determine the
middle word throughout this strip. A forced value other than zero at a
hole, or other than +1/-1 at a retained vertex, rules out every completion
of these terminal data, regardless of the centre and the remaining colours.

**Proof.** A within-arm distance is at most A. Any distance involving the
third arm is at most A+C. Thus a pair at distance A+B-k, 0<=k<K,
uses the first two arms, and its offsets sum to k. For two retained
vertices u,v, twice the contribution to the difference of the two distance
profiles is s(u)+s(v): it is 2 for two positive signs, -2 for two negative
signs and zero for opposite signs. If either vertex is omitted the
contribution is zero. The generating polynomial for exactly these
contributions is therefore SV+UQ, modulo z^K. Setting it to zero and
multiplying by 1-z gives (1). Conversely 1-z has constant term 1 and is
invertible modulo z^K, so (1) makes every one of these shells vanish.
This also accounts for holes: U and V are occupancies, not sign words.

Finally 1-(1-z)H_L has constant term 1. Comparing coefficients in (1)
recursively gives the unique answer

```text
q_k = -s_k
      + sum_(h in H_M) (s_(k-h)-s_(k-h-1))
      + sum_(h in H_L) (q_(k-h)-q_(k-h-1)),                    (2)
```

where negative subscripts are zero. Every q on the right has index below
k, because h>=1. This proves uniqueness over the integers, with no search,
periodicity, or sign assumption used in the algebra. If both strips have
no holes, (2) says q_k=-s_k for all k<K. QED.

## Uniform consequence for the requested ratio

A target-or-larger pair on T(50t,33t,9t) omits at most three vertices:
its union has at least 2(46t-1)=92t-2 of the 92t+1 vertices. The last
retained depths are 50t-e_L,33t-e_M,9t-e_S, where the e's count consecutive
omitted tips and have sum at most three. Their ordering stays strict for
every t>=1. Hence

```text
K = 24t-e_M+e_S >= 24t-3.
```

The theorem reconstructs that many middle-arm terminal signs for **every**
possible pair, including those with holes on different arms or at the centre.
It removes at least 24t-3 independent sign choices after holes and long-arm
signs have been fixed. It does not assert that the reconstructed signs are
permitted, that lower shells agree, or that a completion exists.

## The first scale and the remaining step

The t=1 witness uses centre sign minus and these depth words; zero marks an
omitted vertex, through which distances still travel:

```text
long   +--+-++++-+--+-0+----++++++----++----++++++----++0
middle -++++------++++--++++------++++--
short  --++++--0
```

The omitted vertices are long-arm depths 16 and 50, and short-arm depth 9.
The central-path word is not reflection-antisymmetric. This is why the
previous reflection-based admission obstruction does not forbid it.
For these holes K=25; the last 25 long/middle signs are opposite, as the
new theorem requires. At t=2, the positive control has holes at long depths
33 and 100 and short depth 18, and K=49; it is Rook's r|b square wave.

The unresolved step is to satisfy the lower-distance shells and the sign
constraints uniformly for all remaining t, possibly moving the omissions.
The terminal recurrence is a reduction of that problem, not its solution.
In scratch work a bounded solver reported infeasibility at scales 3 through 6 with
holes fixed at (long 17t-1, long 50t, short 9t). This is only a finite,
fixed-hole search outcome, not an obstruction to the family; it is not used
in any proof or exposed as an impossibility certificate. No default selector,
Gallery entry, runtime, or earlier construction was changed.
