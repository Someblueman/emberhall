# A tripod construction, including every 50:33:9 scale

Tern, 21 November 2026. For every pair of integers **p>=1, q>=0**,

```text
T(15p+5q, 10p+3q, 3p)
```

has disjoint homometric sets of size **14p+4q-1**. The only omitted
vertices are the three arm tips. Setting **p=3t, q=t** proves the requested
size **46t-1** on **T(50t,33t,9t)** for every positive integer t.
The universal milestone for arbitrary triples remains open; external
[priority remains provisional](PRIORITY_REFRESH.md).

Equivalently, this covers positive integer lengths satisfying
9a+5c=15b and a>=5c, and their arm relabelings. The equation implies
3 divides c and 5 divides a; then p=c/3 and q=a/5-c recover the parameters.
This is a sufficient domain, not a classification of all possible pairs.

The general block and companion results below cover further domains.

## Literal words and size

Put d=3p+q. Give the centre sign minus. On each arm, starting at depth 1,
concatenate the following signed runs; a zero-length run contributes nothing:

```text
long:   +(d-1), -p, +p, -q, +p, -p, +(p+q), -p, +p, -q, +p, -p, +d
middle: -(d-1), +p, -p, +q, -p, +p, -d
short:  -(p-1), +p, -p
```

Here `+k` means k positive vertices and `-k` means k negative vertices,
not an arithmetic addition to the preceding run. Each word stops one vertex
before its original arm tip. Thus its length is respectively 15p+5q-1,
10p+3q-1, 3p-1. All other vertices, including the centre, receive exactly one
sign; the positive and negative sets are disjoint.

The sums of signs on the three arms are respectively
7p+q-1, -6p-q+1, -p+1. Together with the centre's -1 they sum to zero.
There are 28p+8q-2 retained vertices, so both sets have 14p+4q-1 vertices.
The original order is 28p+8q+1, making this precisely floor(order/2)-1.
Only tips are omitted, so retained-vertex distances agree with those in the
full prefix tripod of the three displayed word lengths. No edge is contracted.

## The complete distance identity

For a fully retained tripod of lengths l_i, centre sign s0, and signed depth
polynomials S_i(z), assume total sign zero. Write star for replacing z by z^-1.
Twice the difference of the two distance profiles is the signed sum of the
full distance enumerators of the vertices. At depth j on arm i, that enumerator
is U_j+U_(l_i-j)+sum_(k!=i) z^j U_(l_k), with U_m=z+...+z^m.
For the centre it is sum_i U_(l_i). Multiplying by 1-z, summing with signs,
and using total sign zero gives z times

```text
Q = s0(1-sum_i z^l_i)
    + sum_i S_i(1-sum_(j!=i) z^l_j) - sum_i z^l_i S_i* .          (3)
```

Consequently Q=0 is equivalent to equality of every distance multiplicity,
not only selected moments. This reconstructs the full-shell identity used in
[PUBLISHED_CASES.md](PUBLISHED_CASES.md#a-polynomial-form-of-the-full-shell-equations).

Here is an exact identity before any parameter substitution. In the Laurent
ring Z[z,z^-1,x,x^-1,y,y^-1], set

```text
F=1-x+x^2, V=x^2 y, H=x^5 y^2, E=x^10 y^3, C=x^3,
K=2x^3 y F(1-V), D=2x-2x^2+x^3,
E_L=HE, E_M=E, E_S=C,
N_L=z-(1+H)K-HE, N_M=-z+K+E, N_S=-z+D.
```

Substitute x=z^p, y=z^q. Summing each run as a geometric interval shows that
N_i=(1-z)S_i, and E_i=z^(l_i+1). This remains true when q=0 or p=1: empty
runs cancel at their common endpoints. Star now inverts all three variables.
Since (1-z)S_i*=-z N_i*, multiplying (3) by z(1-z) gives

```text
R = -(1-z)(z-E_L-E_M-E_S)
    + sum_i N_i(z-sum_(j!=i) E_j) + z sum_i E_i N_i*.             (4)
```

To verify R=0 by hand, collect powers of z. The z^2 terms cancel; the constant
and linear coefficients are

```text
R0 = E[2H+K(1+V^-1)+C(H-1)-D(H+1)],
R1 = -1-HK+D-C-HE K*+C D*.
```

Use K(1+V^-1)=2xF-2HF and D=2xF-C. Then
R0=2HE[1-(1+x)F+C]=0, because (1+x)F=1+C.
Also HE K*=-HK and D-C+C D*=1, so R1=0. This proves (4) identically.
The executable independently expands the endpoint polynomials and (4) over
integer coefficients; it does not assume these cancellations.

After substitution, z(1-z) is a nonzero Laurent polynomial. Cancellation in
this integral domain gives Q=0, hence equality of all original-tree distance
multiplicities. Together with the explicit coverage and size argument above,
this proves the construction for every p>=1, q>=0.

## A general block family

The same argument allows both trains and short-arm bands to repeat. For
integers **p>=1, k>=1, m>=0, r>=2kp**, there are target-sized pairs on

```text
T((2k+3)p+(4m+1)r, (2k+2)p+(2m+1)r, (2k+1)p),
```

of size **3(k+1)p+(3m+1)r-1**. The preceding construction is k=m=1,
r=2p+q. The CLI uses `--parameters P Q --bands K --layers M`, with
r=2KP+Q, so nonnegative Q enforces the domain.

```sh
python3 projects/almost-halving-three-branches/ratio_50_33_9.py --parameters 1 1 --bands 3 --layers 2
```

This recorded run gives lengths (72,43,7), equal size and target 60,
and 1770 unordered distances on each side. The independent graph tests
also cover m=0, the minimum step r=2kp, and fresh larger k and m.

Here are the exact words and proof, without relying on a pattern fitted
to finite outputs. Set x=z^p, V=z^r, W=V^(2m), C=x^(2k+1), and

```text
F=1-x+x^2-...+x^(2k), P=x-x^2+...-x^(2k),
H=xW, E=x C V W,
K=2x V F (1-V+V^2-...-V^(2m-1)), D=C+2P.
```

The V sum is empty for m=0. Keep the same forms
N_L=z-(1+H)K-HE, N_M=-z+K+E, N_S=-z+D.
Then E_L=HE, E_M=E, E_S=C are exactly the claimed original arm lengths.
The coefficient sequence of N_i/(1-z), from depth 1 to one before its
endpoint, is the actual sign word implemented by the command.

To prove these coefficients are signs, each j=1,...,2m contributes to K
an alternating string of 2k+1 jumps, at jr+p,jr+2p,...,jr+(2k+1)p.
The jump coefficients are 2(-1)^(j-1), -2(-1)^(j-1), and so on. Successive
strings are ordered because r>=2kp; opposite jumps cancel when endpoints
coincide at equality. Starting from the middle arm's -1, odd strings
alternate to end at +1, and even strings alternate to end at -1.
After the even number of strings the sign is again -1.
The long arm starts at +1 and has the negative of this train followed by
a second copy shifted by p+2mr. The second starts r-(2k-1)p>=p positions
after the first ends, so it too stays in {+1,-1}. The short word consists
of 2k+1 alternating blocks, starting with p-1 negative vertices and then
p positive, p negative, and so on, ending negative. Endpoint jumps bring
each word to zero at its omitted tip, with no internal omission.

At z=1, K/(1-z) has coefficient sum 2mr (expand
(1-V^(2m))/(1+V) to first order and use F(1)=1). The arm sign sums are
(2k+3)p+r-1, -(2k+2)p-r+1, -p+1. With the centre's -1 these sum to zero.
Half the retained order is the stated 3(k+1)p+(3m+1)r-1.

Finally use F=(1+C)/(1+x), P=(x-C)/(1+x) and
K=2xVF(1-W)/(1+V). The same R0 and R1 vanish:
K(1+V^-1)=2xF(1-W), D=2xF-C, HE K*=-HK,
D-C+C D*=1, and (1+x)F=1+C. These identities require neither k nor m
to be fixed. The proof command independently expands
**(1+x)(1+V)R in five independent variables z,x,V,W,C** after clearing
the two denominators; it is the zero Laurent polynomial.
Specializing C=x^(2k+1) and W=V^(2m) makes both quotients the finite
alternating sums displayed above. This proves the whole stated domain,
including m=0 and r=2kp. No finite census is used in the proof.

A useful arithmetic recognition is available for sorted lengths a>b>c.
Set r=2b-a-c>0, m=floor((a-b)/(2r)), and p=a-b-2mr.
If p>0, c/p is an odd integer at least three, and r>=c-p, then
k=(c/p-1)/2 and q=r-2kp are in the proved domain. This is a sufficient
construction test, not a negative result for triples failing it.

## A companion family

The same signed-shell identity gives another domain: for integers
**1<=p<r**, T(7r+p,5r,2r-p) has two disjoint homometric sets of size
**7r-1**, omitting only the three tips. For example:

```sh
python3 projects/almost-halving-three-branches/ratio_50_33_9.py --companion 3 7
```

This recorded run returns lengths (52,35,11), size and target 48, and 1128
unordered distances per side. Independent tests include the boundary r=p+1
and further fresh parameters, as well as a corrupted polynomial rejection.

Use centre sign minus and these runs:

```text
long:   +(2r-p-1), -p, +r, -p, +(r-p), -p, +r, -p, +(2r-p)
middle: -(2r-p-1), +p, -r, +p, -(2r-p)
short:  -(r-p-1), +p, -(r-p)
```

All lengths are nonnegative in the stated domain. They total respectively
7r+p-1,5r-1,2r-p-1. Their sign sums are 7r-7p-1,4p-5r+1,3p-2r+1,
which sum to 1; the centre cancels it. The retained order is 14r-2.

For the full distance proof, set x=z^p, V=z^r, H=xV^2, E=V^5,
C=V^2/x, P=V(x^-1-1), D=C+2P, and

```text
K=2V^2(x^-1-1)+2V^3(1-x).
```

The words have N_L=z-(1+H)K-HE, N_M=-z+K+E, N_S=-z+D.
Here E=HCV, P+C P*=0 and
K(1+V^-1)=2[(C-H)+(H+1)P]. The latter follows by multiplying out
(C-H)+(H+1)P=V(1-x)(1+V)(1+xV)/x.
As before R=R0+zR1. The displayed relation makes R0 zero directly.
The antisymmetry of P gives D-C+C D*=1 and E K*=-K, making R1 zero.
Thus every distance multiplicity agrees. `--prove` also expands this
companion identity independently over integer Laurent polynomials.

## An additional all-pairs reduction: arbitrary terminal holes

This independent lemma was obtained before the construction. Consider any
tripod whose last retained arm depths, in decreasing order, are A>=B>C>0.
Set k=B-C. Let s_j,q_j be the signs at depths A-j,B-j for 0<=j<k, including
zero at omitted vertices. Let H_L,H_M list omitted offsets in these strips.
Their constant coefficients are zero, because the retained tips are occupied.
Put S=sum s_j z^j and W=sum q_j z^j. The corresponding unsigned occupancy
polynomials are U=1/(1-z)-H_L and V=1/(1-z)-H_M, modulo z^k.

Only pairs across the first two arms can have distance greater than A+C.
Twice their profile difference at distance A+B-j is the j-th coefficient
of SV+UW. Since 1-z is invertible modulo z^k, all these shells agree iff

```text
S+W = (1-z)(S H_M+W H_L)                         (mod z^k).       (5)
```

It follows that the long word and the two hole sets uniquely determine the
middle word, by the integer recurrence

```text
q_j = -s_j + sum_(h in H_M)(s_(j-h)-s_(j-h-1))
            + sum_(h in H_L)(q_(j-h)-q_(j-h-1)),
```

where negative subscripts are zero. All q terms on the right have smaller
index. A forced nonzero at a hole or a value outside {+1,-1} at an occupied
vertex rules out every completion, regardless of the other colours.
If both hole patterns agree, (5) forces W=-S throughout the strip.

Every target-or-larger pair on T(50t,33t,9t) omits at most three vertices.
Its terminal deletions e_L,e_M,e_S have sum at most three and do not change
the arm ordering. Thus k=24t-e_M+e_S>=24t-3: at least that many middle-arm
signs are determined without any reflection, periodicity or fixed-omission
assumption. `--lemma T` reports this bound without allocating a graph.
The terminal certificate in `--check` is necessary, not sufficient; lower
shells still matter. Exhaustive small colourings independently test exactly
this equivalence, including interior and terminal holes.
