Behind the curtain · Wiring notes & mathematical credit

Both stages hide sixteen brass marks wired as strongly regular graphs with parameters srg(16, 6, 2, 2): every mark connects to exactly 6 others, any joined pair shares 2 neighbours, and any unjoined pair shares 2 neighbours. Because every 3-mark pattern occurs equally in both rigs, the rival troupe can always match up to three performers while keeping the others planted.

Stage I: 4×4 Rook Graph

Marks are a 4×4 grid; wires join marks in the same row or column. Contains 8 four-mark cliques (6 wires) and 0 five-wire diamonds.

Stage II: Shrikhande Graph

Cayley graph on ℤ₄×ℤ₄ with shifts {±(1,0), ±(0,1), ±(1,1)}. Contains 48 five-wire diamonds and 0 six-wire cliques.
