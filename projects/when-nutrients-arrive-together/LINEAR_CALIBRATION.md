# Exact stationary linear calibration

Sorrel, 26 November 2026. This extends the clean-reference investigation;
it does not complete milestone 1 or replace the failed nonlinear gates.

## Question and construction

Can finite record length alone explain a substantial gap between our stationary
linear prediction and the short nonlinear study? Use the same twelve community
matrices, four fresh independent paths each, and the same fixed-segment estimator.
No correlated-input or reserved held-out experiment is involved.

For the continuous augmented linear system dZ = A Z dt + D dW, stationary
covariance P solves A P + P A.T + D D.T = 0. Unit-time exact sampling gives
Z_next = F Z + epsilon with F = exp(A), epsilon covariance V = P - F P F.T.
Start Z from N(0,P). Then Cov(Z_next) = F P F.T + V = P and the lag covariance
is F P. Thus there is no burn-in, forcing interpolation or ODE stepping error.
The sampler implements these identities directly, independently of the Welch
quadratic-form prediction. Gaussian perturbations are a local-linear reference,
not positive-resource nonlinear ecological trajectories.

All twelve communities 4–15 received four paths of 131072 observations; prefixes
2048, 4096 and 20000 were also scored. Seeds and source hashes were written
before sampling and are preserved in [linear-calibration-protocol.json](linear-calibration-protocol.json).
Paths are averaged within communities before Student-t intervals are formed;
n remains twelve, not forty-eight. Prefixes of one path are dependent. The
intervals are approximate and marginal, not simultaneous. NumPy and SciPy
versions are the same as [clean-v1-protocol.json](clean-v1-protocol.json); no dependency was added.

## Numerical and independent controls

All 48 paths succeeded; retained failures: 0.
Maximum transition covariance residual: 1.21e-17.
Smallest innovation covariance eigenvalue relative to its spectral scale: 3.12e-05.
Any tiny negative covariance eigenvalues within relative 1e-9 are explicitly
reported and truncated as roundoff; a more negative eigenvalue fails. This is
a numerical factorization convention, not a repair of ecological input paths.
A separate two-state control checks stationary covariance and lag covariance.
A delayed coloured-signal control is generated by independent SciPy filters,
with an independently derived delay-line covariance. Its full complex Welch
cross-spectrum agrees with the analytical expected spectrum within the fixed
3.5% normalized tolerance. This supplements the earlier exact white-noise and
40,000-record periodogram controls. The project suite now passes 26 checks.

## Finite length versus the limiting prediction

The table reports mean absolute overlap-rank error against each community’s
infinite-record fixed-segment prediction, first averaged over its four paths.
These are empirical errors across the fixed development sample, not rigorous
finite-sample bounds or a guarantee for unseen communities.

| Observations | Correlation | Absolute correlation | Cross-power | Coherence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2048 | 0.22719 | 0.20440 | 0.12595 | 0.04616 |
| 4096 | 0.16776 | 0.15525 | 0.11143 | 0.02855 |
| 20000 | 0.06592 | 0.07483 | 0.05512 | 0.01187 |
| 131072 | 0.02955 | 0.04038 | 0.02519 | 0.00655 |

The mean overlap-rank scores themselves evolve as follows.

| Observations | Correlation | Absolute correlation | Cross-power | Coherence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2048 | 0.68736 | 0.63735 | 0.80930 | 0.91611 |
| 4096 | 0.74858 | 0.69952 | 0.82486 | 0.93647 |
| 20000 | 0.85250 | 0.79549 | 0.89174 | 0.95523 |
| 131072 | 0.89452 | 0.82765 | 0.92576 | 0.95832 |
| Infinite-record prediction | 0.91142 | 0.83713 | 0.93524 | 0.95966 |

The 4096-observation stationary-linear correlation score is close to the
nonlinear study’s mean 0.7483. This demonstrates that substantial finite-record
loss can occur without forcing-grid error, numerical integration error, or a
nonstationary start. It does not prove that nonlinear effects or interpolation
are negligible, nor identify every source of error in the nonlinear run.
Even at 131072 observations, absolute-correlation overlap ranks retain visible
error; convergence is not certified by this simulation.

All three targets, intervals and prediction errors are retained separately in
[linear-calibration-summary.csv](linear-calibration-summary.csv): overlap rank,
pair-label AUC and single-linkage guild AUC. Do not infer ranking accuracy from
perfect observed guild classification or treat paths as independent communities.

## Segment length changes the estimand

A deterministic sensitivity calculation uses the same twelve community matrices
and the already specified segment lengths 16, 30 and 60. Even with infinitely
many stationary observations, changing segment length changes the Hann window
and constant-detrending operation, not merely a numerical integration grid.
The correlation prediction is invariant; the spectral predictions need not be.
No observed outcome selects a preferred segment.

| Segment | Measure | Mean predicted overlap rank | Largest per-community rank change versus segment 30 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 16 | cpsd | 0.94820 | 0.07328 |
| 16 | coherence | 0.96085 | 0.01786 |
| 30 | cpsd | 0.93524 | 0.00000 |
| 30 | coherence | 0.95966 | 0.00000 |
| 60 | cpsd | 0.92617 | 0.03214 |
| 60 | coherence | 0.96026 | 0.00357 |

All three targets are retained in [linear-segment-sensitivity.csv](linear-segment-sensitivity.csv).
Replay with `linear_segment_sensitivity.py --output DESTINATION`. This is an
exact fixed-window model sensitivity up to linear-algebra roundoff, not a
claim that a finer segment must improve the nonlinear estimator. The clean
protocol must name its estimator before interpreting record-length convergence.
