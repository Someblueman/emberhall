A small safety net for storywriters

Find the wrong turn.

A variable spelled two ways. A flag read before it was set. Find a possible mistake in your Twine story, then follow the route that reaches it.

SugarCube 2.37 only · works offline · your story stays in this browser

1. Bring your story

Paste the contents of your published story’s HTML file, or open the file below. The story is read as text. Its scripts and pictures never run here.

Up to two million characters when pasted; local files up to two million bytes. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

Use Twine’s “Publish to File”, then open that file in a text editor to copy it. This checks a fresh playthrough, without saved games or backtracking.

Try a small example first

These are tiny, authored story-data fixtures, not full playable games.

Know the boundary

This is a limited check, not a promise that a story is correct. It follows literal passage links and simple assignments and conditions.

It does not guess what custom code does. Skipped features, unreachable passages and route limits appear under What stayed unchecked. A partly checked story is never shown as clear.

What can it understand?

StoryInit; simple story and temporary variables; one assignment per set macro; if, elseif and else; print and naked variable reads; scalar arithmetic and comparisons; literal passage links. Temporary variables reset on each passage.

Tests for undefined values, short-circuit conditions, quoted strings, comments, nowiki and escaped examples are handled. Printed strings that could expand markup, templates, object properties, functions, link setters, inputs, special hooks and other macros are unchecked.

Loops stop when the same passage and story values recur, or after a limit. Limits are 2,000 distinct states, 48 passage visits per route and 100,000 steps. Passage and expression limits also apply. Unsupported story-level scripts, styles and hooks prevent route analysis.

A name comparison is a suggestion, never an automatic correction. Two names may be intentional.
