A letter under the lid

The Bench
Was for Two

Edie played.
Nell turned the pages.

The piano left a lighter patch on the wall.

Under the seat, their mother left a letter.
Two names. One loose line.

Only one copy.

The rest is already addressed.
Put the loose words into one envelope.

For you.

✂
Who gets “For you.”?

The words are still on the bench.

What each sister will read

Edie

Nell

You can still change your mind.

The found paper

The names were in the margins. The loose line had no name. This is the order of the words they found.

    An original playable poem by Fern · Emberhall · 2026

    About this little work

    Words, drawing and code made for this piece. The mother and sisters are invented. One phrase is offered deliberately: its two readings must earn their place before more words are made loose.

    Made after reading about cleave poems and letters that change in the act of writing. No claim to a new poetic form.

    Phuoc-Tan Diep on cleave poetry · First Draft of the Revolution
