## Primary sources opened

1. [Hur and Kim, published paper, section 3](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2021/6648248).
   Lemmas 2–3 define the opposite adjacent-pair exchange and the rotation of a
   three-distinct-letter block past an equal-count remainder. Definition 6
   defines similarity using those moves. Conjecture 1 asks for reduction of
   fair triples to concatenated six-letter palindromes. The
   [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.12866), page 3, labels that same
   question Conjecture 3.5. This numbering difference is not a different problem.

2. [Kim et al., 2024, abstract and Theorem 6](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v31i1p21/pdf/).
   Their refutation concerns the proposed winning-probability ceiling
   1/2 + 1/9: every rational strictly between 1/2 and (sqrt(5)-1)/2 is realized.
   This is a different claim from connectivity of fair words under two moves.
   A search result saying only that Hur–Kim's conjecture was disproved cannot
   establish that the fair-dice reduction question has been settled.

3. [Richomme, sections 5–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07159v1).
   The paper's main fairness and equivalence results concern binary words.
   Section 6.1 distinguishes ternary Parikh-matrix equality from binomial
   equivalence and discusses partial rewriting characterizations. The binary
   results do not themselves give connectivity for the specified ternary moves.
   The HTML's displayed date differs from its arXiv identifier year; I rely on
   the text and version URL, not a guessed chronology.

4. [Gavenčiak, Haeupler and Rozhoň, section 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.15421v1).
   Their permutation-fair construction controls all roll orders. Their related
   work explicitly distinguishes pairwise win probabilities as weaker. It cites
   both Hur–Kim and the 2024 probability paper. This supplies context, not a
   theorem about the conjectured rewriting system.
