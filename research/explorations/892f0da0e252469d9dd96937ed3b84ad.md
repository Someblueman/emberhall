## Open scientific question

In *Median and small parsimony problems on RNA trees* (*Bioinformatics*,
Vol. 40, Suppl. 1, July 2024, pp. i237–i246; `10.1093/bioinformatics/btae229`,
`PMC11256950`, `arXiv:2402.03455`), Bertrand Marchand, Yoann Anselmetti, Manuel
Lafond, and Aïda Ouangraoua represent aligned non-coding RNA (ncRNA) secondary
structures on a common leafset $L = \{1, \dots, n\}$ as ordered rooted trees
and study the **Median** and **Small Parsimony** problems across three
distances ($d_{\mathrm{RF}}$, $d_{\mathrm{IL}}$, $d_{\mathrm{RE}}$) and four
structural constraints ($C \in \{\mathrm{NC}, \mathrm{DLC}, \mathrm{ILC},
\mathrm{BPC}\}$).

While they establish polynomial-time exact algorithms for $\mathrm{RF}_{\mathrm{NC}}$,
$\mathrm{RF}_{\mathrm{DLC}}$, $\mathrm{IL}_{\mathrm{ILC}}$ Median
($O(p \cdot n^3)$), and $\mathrm{IL}_{\mathrm{NC}}$ Median ($O(p \cdot n^5)$),
they explicitly leave **$\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median** and **$\mathrm{RE}_C$ Small
Parsimony** open:

- On [PMC11256950](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11256950/) (and
  [arXiv:2402.03455](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03455)):
  > "The approach inspired by Feijao and Meidanis (2011) cannot be applied to
  > the $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Small Parsimony either because the Fitch-Hartigan
  > method cannot be applied to compute a minimum presence/absence (0/1)
  > assignment for each base pair, independently of other base pairs. Indeed,
  > two different base pairs from two distinct input trees can be mapped in an
  > optimal assignment of RNA trees to ancestral nodes. Therefore, base pairs
  > cannot be considered independently of each other. The complexity of the
  > $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Small Parsimony remains open."
- On [10.1093/bioinformatics/btae229](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae229):
  > "Although we know of no good algorithm for the $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Small
  > Parsimony or Median problems, we can infer an assignment of RNA trees to
  > ancestors in the phylogeny $T$, with the requirement that each ancestor is
  > assigned one of the RNA trees that appears at the leaves, and the SPcost of
  > the assignment is minimal as in the first step of the previous
  > $\mathrm{IL}_C$ heuristic."

## Formal definitions verified from the primary paper

From `PMC11256950` and `arXiv:2402.03455`:

1. **RNA trees and valid mappings.** An RNA secondary structure $R$ on aligned
   positions $\{1, \dots, n\}$ has internal base-pair nodes $I(R)$ ordered by
   sequence position ($<$) and nesting ($\prec$). A valid tree-edit mapping
   $\mathcal{M} \subseteq I(R_1) \times I(R_2)$ is a bijection between subsets
   of internal nodes preserving both $<$ ($x_1 < y_1 \iff x_2 < y_2$) and
   $\prec$ ($x_1 \prec y_1 \iff x_2 \prec y_2$).
2. **Relaxed Edit ($\mathrm{RE}$) distance.** For cost function
   $c^*((i_1, j_1), (i_2, j_2)) = |i_1 - i_2| + |j_1 - j_2|$ (and $0$ when
   identical):
   $$\mathrm{cost}_{c^*}(\mathcal{M}) = \sum_{(x_1, x_2) \in \mathcal{M}} c^*(x_1, x_2) + |I(R_1)| + |I(R_2)| - 2|\mathcal{M}|,$$
   and $d_{\mathrm{TE}}^{c^*}(T_1, T_2) = \min_{\mathcal{M} \in \mathcal{M}(T_1, T_2)} \mathrm{cost}_{c^*}(\mathcal{M})$.
   Because leaving two nodes unmapped contributes $1 + 1 = 2$, any shift with
   $|i_1 - i_2| + |j_1 - j_2| \ge 2$ never improves on deletion plus insertion.
3. **Constraint families $C$.** Relative to input RNA trees $T_1, \dots, T_p$:
   - $\mathrm{NC}$: any valid non-crossing RNA secondary structure on $L$;
   - $\mathrm{BPC}$: base pairs restricted to $\bigcup_i BP(T_i)$;
   - $\mathrm{DLC}$: descendant leafsets restricted to $\bigcup_i DL(T_i)$;
   - $\mathrm{ILC}$: internal-node leaf-children restricted to $\bigcup_i IL(T_i)$.

## Diagnostic structural checks (author sanity checks, not a project result)

Two elementary 3-leaf checks verified directly in Python during this exploration
illustrate why Marchand et al.'s leaf-tree-only fallback and input-pair
restrictions cannot match true $\mathrm{RE}_C$ optima:

1. **Novel hybrid pair under $\mathrm{RE}_{\mathrm{NC}}$ ($n=8, p=3$).** For
   $T_1 = \{(1,6)\}$, $T_2 = \{(2,7)\}$, and $T_3 = \{(1,8)\}$, every pairwise
   distance among the leaves is $2$, so any leaf-only or $\mathrm{BPC}$ median
   costs $4$. The novel non-input pair $M = \{(1,7)\}$ has $d_{\mathrm{RE}}(M, T_k) = 1$
   for all $k \in \{1,2,3\}$, giving total median cost $3 < 4$.
2. **Suboptimality of the leaf-only heuristic even under $\mathrm{RE}_{\mathrm{BPC}}$
   ($n=11, p=3$).** For $T_1 = \{(1,4), (7,11)\}$, $T_2 = \{(1,4), (8,10)\}$,
   and $T_3 = \{(2,5), (7,10)\}$, the input trees achieve median totals $5$, $5$,
   and $6$, whereas $M = \{(1,4), (7,10)\}$ uses only input base pairs (valid
   for $\mathrm{BPC}$, $\mathrm{DLC}$, and $\mathrm{NC}$) and achieves total
   cost $1 + 1 + 2 = 4 < 5$.

These toy checks motivate the real question for the proposed project: what is
the exact structural and algorithmic boundary between tractable subcases (e.g.,
small $p$, bounded shift radius, or exact branch-and-bound / DP) and true
computational intractability under $\mathrm{RE}_C$?

## Closest existing work read and limits

- [Marchand, Anselmetti, Lafond & Ouangraoua 2024 (PMC11256950)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11256950/)
  and [DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btae229](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae229)
  and [arXiv:2402.03455](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03455): introduces
  $d_{\mathrm{RE}}$ ($d_{\mathrm{TE}}^{c^*}$), proves polynomial-time exact
  algorithms for $\mathrm{RF}_{\mathrm{NC}}$, $\mathrm{RF}_{\mathrm{DLC}}$,
  $\mathrm{IL}_{\mathrm{ILC}}$, and $\mathrm{IL}_{\mathrm{NC}}$, and leaves
  $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median and Small Parsimony open, using a leaf-tree-only
  Sankoff-style assignment as a heuristic.
- [bmarchand/rna_small_parsimony](https://github.com/bmarchand/rna_small_parsimony):
  companion Python/Snakemake pipeline evaluating the paper's algorithms and
  heuristics on Rfam 14.10 seed alignments; does not provide an exact solver or
  complexity resolution for $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median or Small Parsimony.
- [Feijão & Meidanis 2011 (PMID 21339538)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21339538/):
  introduces the Single-Cut-or-Join (SCJ) breakpoint-like distance and solves
  SCJ Small Parsimony via a Fitch-Hartigan-style independent-character algorithm;
  Marchand et al. show this fails for $\mathrm{RE}_C$ because distinct shifted
  base pairs can map to one another.
- [Höchsmann, Töller, Giegerich & Kurtz 2003 (PMID 16452790)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16452790/):
  RNAforester tree/forest alignment for pairwise and multiple RNA secondary
  structure comparison; computes alignments/distances rather than solving
  $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median or phylogenetic Small Parsimony.
- [Zhang & Shasha 1989 (SIAM J. Comput.)](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0218082):
  foundational dynamic programming algorithm for pairwise ordered tree edit
  distance; gives the pairwise distance engine inside $d_{\mathrm{RE}}$, not the
  multi-tree median or small parsimony problem.
- [Rfam database](https://rfam.org): curated non-coding RNA families and
  clans with consensus secondary structures used in Marchand et al.'s empirical
  benchmarks.

## Proposed small project (`rna-relaxed-edit-parsimony`)

- **Scale / Field / Worth / For:** `small` / `science` / `enabling` / `field`.
- **Form:** A self-contained mathematical/algorithmic study (`proof.md`,
  `README.md`) paired with a zero-dependency Python standard-library exact
  reference solver and verifier (`python3 projects/rna-relaxed-edit-parsimony/check.py`
  and `unittest` suite), NOT a web app or visualization toy.
- **First version:**
  1. Independently reconstruct and verify the exact $\mathrm{RE}$ distance
     $d_{\mathrm{TE}}^{c^*}$ against brute-force enumeration of all valid
     order- and nesting-preserving mappings $\mathcal{M}$ on small RNA trees.
  2. Build an exact solver for $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median ($p=3, 4$) and small
     $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Small Parsimony trees across $C \in \{\mathrm{NC},
     \mathrm{BPC}, \mathrm{DLC}, \mathrm{ILC}\}$.
  3. Quantify and prove the exact structural separation among $\mathrm{RE}_{\mathrm{NC}}$,
     $\mathrm{RE}_{\mathrm{BPC}}$, and Marchand et al.'s leaf-only heuristic
     (including minimal exact counterexamples and small Rfam-like shifted-stem
     motifs), AND deliver either a proven exact polynomial-time DP for a clearly
     bounded $\mathrm{RE}_C$ case (such as $p=3$ or bounded shift/nesting) or a
     rigorous NP-hardness reduction for $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median / Small Parsimony.
- **Falsifier / when to drop:** Drop if a 2024–2026 follow-up already settles
  the complexity of $\mathrm{RE}_C$ Median and Small Parsimony, if the solver
  only reproduces Marchand et al.'s leaf-only heuristic, or if the project
  produces only heuristic/numerical guesses without exact certificates or a
  bounded theorem.
