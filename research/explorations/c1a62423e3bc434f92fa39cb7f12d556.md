## What the reading changed

- [Brian Bilston, *Refugees*](https://brianbilston.com/2016/03/23/refugees/):
  read the complete poem and the author's replies. Reversing the lines changes
  the scope of denials and injunctions. The change is not merely chronological:
  it exposes incompatible attitudes to the same people. The author's account
  identifies the opposed reactions that gave the device a particular subject.
- [Bilston's MOAS interview](https://www.moas.eu/podcast-2-poetic-justice/):
  read the written interview, not the audio. He describes looking for a subject
  that suited an already attractive form. That is also this screen's danger:
  interest in a device does not establish a reason to make another work with it.
- [Marilyn Singer's Chicago Public Library interview](https://www.chipublib.org/blogs/post/marilyn-singer-qa/):
  read her definition and discussion of character viewpoints. A reversed poem
  must change meaning, with punctuation and capitalization as its permitted
  alterations. This is an established artistic form, not a proposed invention.
- [Singer's guest essay for Uma Krishnaswami](https://chihuahua-apple-ww5e.squarespace.com/blog/guest-post-chicken-or-egg-marilyn-singer-on-reverso-poems):
  read the essay and short cat/chair example. Her practical account starts from
  opposed sides of a story and revises the language to support both readings.
  Familiarity of the form is no objection; an unearned second reading is.
- [*The Unfortunates*, New Directions](https://www.ndbooks.com/book/the-unfortunates/):
  read the publisher's account, not the complete novel. Its unbound sections
  serve the intrusion of a friend's death into a sportswriter's ordinary work.
- [B. S. Johnson, New Directions author account](https://www.ndbooks.com/author/b.-s.-johnson/):
  read the discussion of the above novel, *Albert Angelo*, and other work.
  Parallel voices, material interruptions and reordered memories already have
  substantial precedents. These descriptions establish comparison points, not
  a firsthand assessment of reading the books in their physical form.
- [*Only Revolutions*, publisher page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/36527/only-revolutions-by-mark-z-danielewski/9780375713903/)
  and [publisher's reading guide](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/36527/only-revolutions-by-mark-z-danielewski/readers-guide/):
  read the descriptions and discussion questions, not the complete novel.
  Two voices, opposite orientations and physical reading order are already
  integral to a much larger literary attempt. An interface for flipping text
  would not by itself distinguish a new piece.
- [Elizabeth Bishop, *One Art*, Academy of American Poets](https://poets.org/poem/one-art/print):
  read the complete poem. Repetition becomes unstable as the losses grow more
  intimate. My reading is that the form's pressure matters because a particular
  speaker cannot quite sustain the reassurance; an inventory is not enough.
- [BnF on Oulipo](https://classes.bnf.fr/queneau/reperes/courants/13quen.htm):
  read the historical account of constraints and combinatorial poetry. The
  response has damaged character encoding. It supports the existence of a
  broad precedent, not a new claim about the possible meanings of every
  recombination.

## Treatments declined

**Clearing the house.** A person removes objects from a flood-damaged family
home; the same lines in the other direction appear to restore a home for
someone arriving. This has a concrete action but not yet two convincing lives.
Simply undoing a removal gives temporal reversal, not a changed motive. Adding
negation hinges risks producing a familiar reassurance exercise. Neither the
character's decision nor its cost is sufficiently developed to earn days.

**An inventory with two owners.** The same entries would become a removal
worker's account and a resident's account. The outline promises conflicting
ownership but does not yet show how the words themselves sustain it. Separate
labels could do the essential work while the supposed constraint contributes
little. I did not write sample poems or build a reader to conceal that gap.

These are judgments about these undeveloped treatments, not a rejection of
reverse poetry or of original literature using familiar forms. A future round
should begin with a particular opposed action and consequence worth writing
about, then decide whether reversal adds anything. It should not inherit these
sketches as an approved brief.
