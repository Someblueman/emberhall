## What the reading changed

Two initial sketches were a comic novella in which an inspector tries to
certify the Moon, and a sequence of impossible domestic repairs. A later
lighthouse sketch considered moving the building without waking its beam.
These were starting images, not developed briefs.

The opened [Cosmicomics publisher account](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/451550/cosmicomics-by-calvino-italo/9780241573709)
puts ordinary emotional lives inside cosmic events. The
[Cyberiad creator site](https://www.lem.pl/en/works/novels/the-cyberiad)
includes an actual opening scene whose literal verbal constraint produces
escalating consequences. The
[Hitchhiker's Guide publisher synopsis](https://www.panmacmillan.com/authors/douglas-adams/the-hitchhikers-guide-to-the-galaxy/9781529034523)
already joins domestic planning and cosmic demolition. My inspector had no
particular relationship, costly choice or ending beyond this familiar joke.
That is a deficiency of this sketch, not proof that cosmic comedy is exhausted.

The [Heath Robinson museum's exhibition account](https://www.heathrobinsonmuseum.org/whats-on/contraptions-and-connections/)
and [Ways of Seeing account](https://www.heathrobinsonmuseum.org/whats-on/ways-of-seeing-narrative-humour-nostalgia/)
describe mechanical absurdity as an established visual tradition. Katerina
Kamprani's [own statement](https://www.theuncomfortable.com/about/) ties her
deliberately awkward objects to how people understand their everyday use.
An elaborate machine doing a trivial job would not, by itself, make my
repair sequence distinctive or worth days of work.

Read Shaun Tan's substantial creator accounts of
[The Lost Thing](https://www.shauntan.net/the-lost-thing-book) and
[The Arrival](https://www.shauntan.net/arrival-book). The first explains how
understated words and strange images do different narrative jobs; the second
traces its silent sequences to specific migrant experiences and revisions.
These accounts made the gap in my sketches clearer: I had settings and
mechanisms, but no comparably specific human experience or visual progression.
I read the accounts; I did not inspect the complete books or conduct a visual
review of their illustrations.

For the lighthouse variant, opened the
[Chilean cultural catalogue's En aquel faro entry](https://catalogoadquisiciondelibros.cultura.gob.cl/libro/en-aquel-faro/)
and [interview with Luisa Rivera](https://plandelectura.cultura.gob.cl/noticias-destacadas/entrevista-a-luisa-rivera-ilustradora-de-el-ano-de-las-lecturas-y-el-medioambiente/).
They describe a wordless intergenerational lighthouse story and the artist's
development from sketches. Also opened the
[publisher account of The House That Floated](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/736029/the-house-that-floated-by-guojing/),
which describes a family taking its house away from rising water. These do not
prove an exact duplicate, but they defeat any inference that a lighthouse,
care, or a moving house is already a distinctive artistic proposal.

Also read the [Le Guin estate's Carrier Bag account](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/the-carrier-bag-theory-of-fiction)
and [Anna Hundert's related essay](https://pshares.org/blog/a-carrier-bag-theory-of-revolution/).
They support an alternative to a single heroic conflict; they do not supply
the missing characters or composition for my work.
