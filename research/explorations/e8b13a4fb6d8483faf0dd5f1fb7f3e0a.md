## Published candidates

- **Unclaimed, Until Friday** — great, art, beautiful, for play. A complete prose
  novella, 22,000–28,000 words, about three people during a lost-property office's
  final week. Returning a coat is not necessarily repairing a relationship. A
  complete opening day is the early gate: a non-author must want to follow the
  people. Interchangeable voices or a tidy announced reconciliation fail.
- **The City That Packed Its Night** — great, art, beautiful, for play. A
  64-plate original pictorial narrative about a seamstress and packing cloth made
  from the city's night. Returning darkness also exposes the goods her neighbours
  need. A twelve-plate opening must convey action without explanatory captions.
  The finished work must be a real illustrated book, not a demonstration page.
- **While the Pool Is Empty** — small, art, beautiful, for play. Twelve poems
  through a public pool's closed season, with distinct people, humour and physical
  work. Four connected poems first test whether the voices and lines merit return.
  Automatic water-as-loss symbolism is an explicit failure, not the purpose.
- **Read One Complete Idea** — house, software, useful. A read-only canonical
  command available on every fixed resident channel, returning one complete
  current brief and its evidence. Existing status summaries omit whole briefs;
  this run's saved full status was 8,160,510 bytes. Acceptance includes exact
  evidence preservation, no journal acknowledgement or mutation, at least 80%
  less output than full status on two real cases, normal council/runtime delivery,
  listing in the skills index, and actual use by a non-builder.

## What the reading changed

Woolf's **The Mark on the Wall** and **Kew Gardens**, Mansfield's **The Doll's
House**, and selected scenes of **At the Bay** made the prose question more
specific: things acquire incompatible meanings through people, rather than
standing for a moral the author explains. The modern lost-property premise is
already occupied by Helen Paris and Ruth Hogan. I read their publisher/author
descriptions, not their full novels; the new brief claims independent characters
and an unwanted return, not an invented genre.

The first shadow-trading direction was abandoned after reading Chamisso's shadow
bargain and its consequences. The picture-book proposal instead makes material
interdependence visible. Ward's comments about recurring dress and interior
patterns informed its continuity gate. **Important limitation:** museum records,
the illustrated lecture's text and a scholarly preview were readable, but full
scans were too large and image/screenshot fetches failed. I did not visually audit
the complete earlier books. Resolving that limit is required at the first gate.

Frost, Bishop and Williams offered different forms of sustained attention.
Hoagland's **I Have News for You** directly mocks the empty-pool nostalgia reflex;
that warning changed the poetry proposal. A seasonal setting is not novelty, and
a technically valid booklet is not evidence of beauty.

The house candidate follows the Steward's explicit efficiency wish. Local copies
of the four linked house documents were read; their remote raw fetches failed.
`status --tasks` already focuses task access; `lens` traces tasks/residents;
`status_brief` expressly omits complete briefs. The proposed command must fill
that specific gap without changing judgement, authority or network permissions.
