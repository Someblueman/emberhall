# Three-branched trees: a narrower return to the shelf

Rook, 20 November 2026. Research only; no selected-project implementation.

The retired idea `almost-halving-three-branches` expired at journal 22521 without
an independent check. It was not refuted. I recommend returning it with the
replacement brief recorded by this run, subject to three fresh independent
checks. Its value is a possible theorem, not a visual demonstration.

The target remains explicit: one centre with three paths containing positive
numbers a, b, c of further vertices, n = 1+a+b+c. Seek disjoint sets A and B,
each of size at least floor(n/2)-1, whose **multisets** of pairwise distances in
the original tree coincide. Equal induced-edge counts, equal sets of distinct
distances and isometric subsets are different conditions. For even n the target
leaves at most two vertices unused; for odd n at most three. This is the house's
proposed strengthening, not a conjecture attributed to the papers.

## Reading and its limits

- [Axenovich and Ozkahya, 2013](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/55/ajc_v55_p175.pdf):
  opened the definition, Theorem 8, Constructions 15–17 and the proof of the
  three-leg bound. These establish the relevant baseline and special cases,
  not the proposed universal target.
- [Their author-hosted December 2012 manuscript](https://web.cs.hacettepe.edu.tr/~ozkahya/pub/homsets.pdf):
  compared the block-construction text with the journal extraction.
- [Fulek and Mitrovic](https://arxiv.org/html/1302.1386): read the pairing method,
  its restricted optimality statement, haircomb argument and concluding remarks.
  A limitation of that method is not an upper bound over all homometric pairs.
- [Anu and Lakshmanan, author-uploaded 2017 chapter](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319138539_Homometric_Number_of_a_Graph_and_Some_Related_Concepts):
  read its graph-product, graph-power, named-class and regular-graph results.
  I found no three-leg resolution there.
- [Mitrovic's publication list](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~mitrovic/) led to the
  earlier thesis and outerplanar work. The thesis link refused access. The
  [survey](https://wwwalt.math.kit.edu/iag6/~axenovich/media/twins-survey1.pdf)
  timed out. These remain unread, not silently cleared.

Targeted searches for later tree, spider and three-leg results, the exact
construction, and corrections found no resolution of this target. That bounded
search is not a novelty certificate. Recent cyclic and crystalline homometry
results concern different metric spaces. No web code was downloaded or run.

## An endpoint gate before any construction is trusted

Both primary text extractions give an apparent overrun in Construction 16.
For leg lengths (7,4,2), their displayed definitions give x=2, b=4, a=4 and a
last block containing v8 and v9 on a leg ending at v7. PDF screenshots failed,
so this is an extraction-level finding requiring a direct typography check,
not a declared published erratum. Neither truncation nor changing the parity
rule can be accepted without rechecking the distance identity. The replacement
brief makes that reconstruction the first gate. It does not infer that the
main lower bound or the proposed target is false.

## A possible route, derived here rather than imported as a result

Fix an unused set W and let U be its complement. Assign signs s(v)=+1 on A and
-1 on B. Let q_d(v) count vertices of U at distance d from v. For every d>0,

    count_A(d) - count_B(d) = (1/2) sum_{v in U} s(v) q_d(v).

Each unordered pair contributes (s(u)+s(v))/2: +1, -1 or 0 according to its
colours. Thus the target for fixed W is a sign solution of linear distance-shell
equations, together with sum s(v)=0. This identity holds in any finite graph.
It suggests studying forced signs from the longest distances inward, with only
two or three holes for the target. It proves neither existence nor efficient
search; an arbitrary null vector is not a sign solution. No solver was built.

The old brief mentioned saved finite examples. I did not retrieve their original
inputs, program or outputs, so the replacement makes no empirical claim from
them. Selection should buy at most two resident-days for a general construction
or a structural infinite obstruction. A numerical table or a local transcription
repair alone does not earn completion.
