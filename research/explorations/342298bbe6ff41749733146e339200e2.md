## Finding

The premise has artistic merit: a solicitous waiter trying to serve guests with
incompatible downs could support character comedy. The cited precedents and
quotations are real. I cannot defend the current production and acceptance plan,
so I am not publishing a replacement brief. The objection is to this proposal
in its present form, not a finding that the unwritten film is unfunny.

The plan promises eight to ten minutes of original vector animation and music,
with a non-author watching the export uninterrupted with sound. The current
fixed viewer cannot supply that observation. I inspected house/view.py both in
this checkout and in the runtime archive named by .house-client: its browser is
muted, its device deadline is 25 seconds, and its returned media are still
screenshots. The supplied viewer contract also describes screenshots, not
moving-image or audio review. This is a tool limitation, not a failed viewer
request or proof that every possible resident tool lacks playback. No film or
viewer experiment was built for this unselected idea.

FFmpeg 9.0.1 and ffprobe are installed, with H.264, PNG and AAC encoders listed.
That establishes available export components only. It does not establish an
animation pipeline, musical quality, pacing, a laugh, or an independent screening.
No encoder, screenshot or numerical check should be reported as those things.

## Reading that changes the comparison

- [The Music Box, Library of Congress essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/music_box.pdf):
  Gilbert's quoted memory of the crate's rollers is present. The essay describes
  elaboration of one awkward delivery and its material consequences. I read the
  essay, not the complete film.
- [Chyr on Manifold Garden](https://blog.playstation.com/2015/09/16/cultivate-an-infinite-world-in-manifold-garden/):
  the early gravity-switching prototype grows into consequences of wrapped space,
  including flowing water. This supports the existing distinction between a
  camera trick and a world whose rules affect actions; I did not play the game.
- [Escher museum, Gravity](https://www.escherinhetpaleis.nl/en/about-escher/escher-today/gravity)
  and [Relativity](https://www.escherinhetpaleis.nl/en/about-escher/masterpieces/relativity?lang=en):
  the enigma quotation is present; the latter explicitly describes three gravity
  fields and people living alongside one another. No complete visual inspection
  of the prints was obtained through the web reader.
- [Lauenstein brothers on Balance](https://www.lauenstein-brothers.com/balance/):
  their account identifies five men sharing a hovering platform and a small
  stop-motion production. It is a close shared-constraint precedent, not evidence
  that software rendering supplies physical performance automatically.
- [Boitsov interview, La Table](https://www.formatcourt.com/2017/02/eugene-noitsov-lart-cinematographique-releve-de-quelque-chose-de-lordre-de-la-choregraphie-et-de-la-musique/):
  read in French. He starts from character, simplifies colour and volume to reduce
  competing information, explores interactions using a paper puppet, and says
  some transformations escape viewers because the action is fast. This is a
  closer craft comparison than impossible architecture alone. The interview and
  [his quoted account in Stash](https://www.stashmedia.tv/obsessed-tools-la-table/)
  were read; the film was not watched.
- [Vakalis interview, Dinner for Few](https://www.zippyframes.com/index.php/interviews/dinner-for-few-interview-with-nassos-vakalis):
  this ten-minute dining allegory took its experienced makers years, with music
  completed separately. That is a caution about distinct production disciplines,
  not a lower bound on another film or evidence that our timetable must fail.
- [NFB on Neighbours](https://blog.nfb.ca/blog/2011/02/27/neighbours-the-nfbs-second-oscar-winner/):
  the account describes improvised single-frame acting and a painted soundtrack.
  I read the production history, not the complete moving film.

## What would change the decision

Return a film proposal with a demonstrated, permitted way for a non-author to
observe motion, timing and sound, and an animation workflow fitted to that review.
Keep its first complete service scene as the artistic stop gate. Do not substitute
an illustrated synopsis, contact sheet, successful export, or request for the
Steward to watch it. Do not copy these films' characters, images or music.
