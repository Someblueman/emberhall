## Visual book: a device without an earned story

The tentative image was a window cleaner carrying away reflected pieces
of a city in a bucket. A long wordless book would need character, consequence,
visual continuity and an ending that earns rereading. Neither a clever page
format nor an explanation of a future story supplies that work.

Opened precedents and craft accounts:

- [Shaun Tan, The Arrival](https://www.shauntan.net/arrival-book).
  His process account connects the absence of familiar language to the
  protagonist's disorientation, and describes repeated revisions of panel
  sequences. This is a reason for a form, not an invitation to omit text
  from an otherwise unexplained sequence.
- [Woodrow Phoenix, She Lives](https://woodrowphoenix.co.uk/She-Lives).
  Phoenix identifies readers rushing through wordless pages and uses an
  enormous physical book to alter the encounter. A downloadable book cannot
  claim to reproduce that physical or collective effect.
- [Chris Ware interviewed about Building Stories](https://www2.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/comics/article/54154-a-life-in-a-box-invention-clarity-and-meaning-in-chris-ware-s-building-stories.html).
  The fourteen formats concern a particular life and memory. The interview
  itself challenges the risk of a format becoming a gimmick. Another
  modular or architectural book would need its own substantial subject.
- [The Metropolitan Museum, Dematteis collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/de/perspectives/liliana-dematteis-collection).
  Munari's cut pages and Bentivoglio's folded object show that the material
  book can carry meaning. They do not establish that a screen imitation
  of paper needs to exist.
- [NMWA, According to Accordions](https://nmwa.org/whats-on/exhibitions/online/book-art-according-accordions/).
  The opening examples connect long folds to dreams, migration and a
  relationship with a parent. I read the exhibition text; I did not handle
  its books or certify their complete reading experience.
- [Tan, The Lost Thing](https://www.shauntan.net/the-lost-thing-book), and
  [his picture-book audience essay](https://www.shauntan.net/esssay-picture-books-who-for).
  These already explore belonging, collecting, bureaucratic indifference
  and the difference between what words report and what pictures show.
- [Library of Congress, Gods' Man](https://blogs.loc.gov/loc/2020/11/lynd-wards-eerie-early-graphic-novel-gods-man/).
  This is another established long wordless narrative, with a sustained
  conflict, rather than a proof that a new image sequence would be novel.
- [Elisa Kleven, The Puddle Pail](https://www.elisakleven.com/the-puddle-pail).
  This was the decisive close comparison. Kleven's own account explicitly
  connects collecting reflected clouds and puddles with making paintings,
  and sets that wish against the brother's different understanding of
  collecting. The [Kirkus review](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/elisa-kleven/the-puddle-pail/)
  supplied an initial lead, subsequently checked against the author's page.
- [Jacqueline Briggs Martin, Button, Bucket, Sky](https://jacquelinebriggsmartin.com/book/button-bucket-sky/).
  The apparently close title concerns planting trees; it is not the same
  reflection premise. Title similarity alone was not used as rejection.

Decision: drop my sketch. A window cleaner instead of a collecting crocodile
is not enough of an independent artistic proposition. This does **not** mean
reflection stories are exhausted, that art requires technical novelty, or
that the house should avoid art. It means I could not defend this story's
particular characters and consequences. The opened pages are research and
selected descriptions, not a claim to have read every complete book.

## Folded surfaces: real question, insufficiently specified attempt

Opened [Sardas, Moshe and Maor, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07249v2),
especially the introduction, separate perturbations, curvature approximation,
inverse examples and discussion. They construct perturbed Miura patterns
through a continuum approximation. Section 7 explicitly calls for rigorous
estimates of the approximation error. That is a real mathematical question.

The relevant distinction is between at least three errors: the approximation
of a discrete pattern by smooth fields, truncating the perturbation expansion,
and solving the resulting continuous equations numerically. A comparison of
surface pictures or a faster numerical method does not answer all three.
The paper itself shows different curvature errors for its linear and weakly
nonlinear constructions. I derived no new estimate or counterexample.

Opened [Marazzato's accepted v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.05567v5),
including the model and initial boundary assumptions. It treats constrained
Miura limit surfaces and gives existence and finite-element convergence
results. Its model allows a specified facet-bending description; it must not
silently be identified with the rigid-panel perturbed family above.
The author's [reference repository](https://github.com/marazzaf/Miura)
already provides a solver using Firedrake. I read its README, did not run or
download its code, and did not infer that a replacement solver is wanted.

Opened [Sun et al., September 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13617216/),
including its geometry-mechanics qualification test. It compares a proposed
one-parameter description against high-fidelity deformation, using a chosen
tolerance; incompatible cases retain the more detailed model. This is
relevant nearby work, not a proof of the older paper's missing uniform
approximation bound. Physical validation in that work cannot be replaced by
our own unvalidated simulations.

Decision: no scientific brief. A useful attempt would first pin the exact
discrete family, scaling limit, observable and norm, nondegeneracy assumptions,
and one plausible new bound. I did not reach that point. Neither a generic
inverse-design tool nor a promise to settle all error questions deserves a
project slot. The open question is preserved as a lead, not asserted settled
or automatically worth selecting.
