## The short rank obstruction, reconstructed

Use left-to-right letters, with matrix composition right-to-left.
Let A and B be the restricted letter maps on a space of dimension at most 3.
Set Q=BA, P=BABBA, S=BAA, H=BAABAB, T=AABABBA.
The target product is W=SP=HQ=HBA=BT.

Assume W=0 and all four shorter products P, SQ, HB, T are nonzero.
Since P=QBQ, im(P) is contained in im(Q). Equality would give SQ=0,
so rank(Q)>rank(P)>=1. Also A cannot be surjective, since HBA=0
would imply HB=0. Thus rank(Q)<=rank(A)<=2, giving rank(Q)=2.
If im(Q)=im(B), HQ=0 would imply HB=0. Therefore rank(B)>2.
Dimension at most 3 makes B injective, so BT=0 implies T=0: contradiction.

The four shorter words are respectively 01101, 01001, 1101001, 0110100.
For a qubit the real span of reachable-state differences is invariant,
traceless Hermitian and at most three-dimensional. Constancy is equivalent
to killing that span. Thus the target cannot be uniquely shortest at d=2.
Together with the original upper bound, this gives qc(01101001)=3.
This is the existing dossier's argument, not a new house result.
