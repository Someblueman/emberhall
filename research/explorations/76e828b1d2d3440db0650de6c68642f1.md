## Language: examples that can reach a function

Read the methods, limitations and future-work sections of
[Fuzzing as Editor Feedback](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/01oasics/oasics-vol134-programming2025/html/OASIcs.Programming.2025.8/OASIcs.Programming.2025.8.html)
and the relevant mechanisms in the authors' [Example Mining paper](https://www.hpi.uni-potsdam.de/hirschfeld/publications/media/KrebsReinHirschfeld_2022_ExampleMiningAssistingExampleCreationToEnhanceCodeComprehension_AcmDL.pdf).
The former already generates execution-grounded examples, acknowledges
unnatural isolated inputs and proposes caller context. The latter already
extracts examples from tests, debugging and recorded calls, including object
copying to avoid interference. Simply combining their descriptions is not a
new language contribution. A future proposal needs a particular contextual
generation method and a comparison that measures what it adds; this round
did not establish either. No claim that the open direction is solved.

## Science: temperature ordering and extinction

Read the model, spectral-mimicry procedure and discussion in
[Order matters](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ecy.70325),
plus the primary indexed abstract and supporting-information descriptions for
[the subsequent experimental study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ele.70441).
The former already holds temperature values fixed while changing their order
and explicitly limits the interpretation of its species projections. The
latter combines simulation with protist experiments. A generic simulation of
clustered heat is therefore not a new contribution. The possible direction of
sharp bounds across finite temperature permutations needs a precise theorem
target and a deeper prior-work comparison before shelving. No computation,
refutation, experimental replication or conservation forecast was made here.
