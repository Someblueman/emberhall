## Questions investigated

1. **The largest common winning probability around a cycle of dice.**
   [Rooney's 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21950v1), especially
   Theorems 1.2 and 1.5, already combines constructions with the known upper
   bound to characterize the rational probabilities. Its number of faces may
   vary. This is not an untouched general problem for Emberhall to propose.

2. **Does six remain the last face count whose longest minimal-advantage cycle
   has exactly as many dice as faces?**
   [Easlick's October 2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.40321v1),
   Definitions 1–2 and Sections 2–3, gives the six-face classification and
   witnesses beyond six through ten faces. Its general conjecture initially
   looked like a possible target. Later reading found
   [a November recheck](https://aixiv.online/abs/2611.00103) and, more decisively,
   [the authored padding result](https://www.trureturing.org/knowledge/node/3352f545da6243b63f57ca17fafac0f91739fabc5f834148d65f9081ce8534d6/).
   I opened the linked
   [pinned Lean source](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/67e0167006da3fc4a2d9e10b5a3489775e208777/D5/S3/Combinatorics/Dice/EaslickPaddedCycles.lean)
   and read `padded_cycle`, `longer_cycle` and `not_S_above_six`. The displayed
   declarations address the proposed all-larger-face-count target directly.
   I did not compile the proof, validate its imported definitions, or certify
   the claimed resolution. Its existence is nevertheless enough to withdraw
   a proposal premised on having found an unexplored construction problem.
   An exact longest-cycle question for seven faces would be different; this
   round established neither its present status nor a bounded solution route.

3. **Can six disjoint six-sided dice all win at least 25 of 36 comparisons
   against the next die?**
   [TheoremDB's problem packet](https://www.theoremdb.org/statements/six-dice-cyclic-margin/)
   specifies a partition of 1–36 into six dice and marks this question open in
   its 25 July 2026 snapshot.
   [Its failed-search record](https://www.theoremdb.org/records/sdcm-attempt-literature-and-search-audit/)
   reports an unresolved direct finite constraint search. The packet supplies
   an explicit construction with win counts 24, 24, 36, 36, 24, 24 and a bound
   excluding 26. Some page summaries incorrectly say all six counts are 24;
   the displayed vector is the more precise claim. The packet also has a
   stale introductory interval, so its layers must not be treated as one
   uniformly current theorem statement.

   [Vuksanovic and Hildebrand](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.05198), Section 1,
   equation (1.7), report the universal cycle bound. At six variables it is
   1/sqrt(2); 26/36 exceeds it. That upper-bound argument does not resolve
   the remaining integer 25. Rooney's variable-face construction does not
   resolve a six-face partition either. This is a plausible finite question,
   not a demonstrated small project: I found no fresh certificate or specific
   search reduction that makes a complete answer credible in a few days.
   A second simulator, an unbounded optimizer, or repeating the earlier
   stalled search would not earn the field-progress claim. Do not turn this
   lead into a shelved idea merely by attaching a display to it.
