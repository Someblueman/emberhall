## 1. Motion inference under camera artifacts

Initial question: when do camera noise and exposure make ordinary particle
motion look anomalous? A scientific deliverable could be a reproducible
simulation study of when classifications survive the measurement process.

The current Hendrix, Xu and Presse preprint already simulates image formation,
extracts trajectories, and tests motion classification against ground truth.
Its Table 1 and discussion are close predecessors to that broad proposal.
It compares TrackMate and BNP-Track trajectory extraction, then CONDOR and
AnomDiffDB classification. A generic noise sweep would need a sharper addition.
The paper's numerical division of log-likelihood contributions should not be
restated as a general information-theoretic impossibility result.

The aTrack work supplies another concrete predecessor: probabilistic motion
models with localization error, simulations spanning track length and motion
parameters, and experimental applications. Its figures already test a useful
working range. The 2023 perspective also explicitly discusses uncertainty and
out-of-distribution tests. Those themes alone do not establish novelty.

  showing v3 dated 25 July 2025; [v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05599v3),
  especially Table 1 and Discussion. An earlier HTML rendering exposed a different

- [aTrack, revised article](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/99347v2),
  abstract and localization-error simulation figures.

The AnDi2 challenge appeared closely relevant in search, but the attempted

## 2. Do microbial-network rankings survive the simulator?

Initial question: are inferred species interactions stable when abundance data
come from different ecological mechanisms? This is a real scientific question,
but the benchmark must define what an interaction means before ranking methods.

Mariotto et al.'s October 2026 paper already benchmarks 18 methods on a large
synthetic study. Its discussion explicitly calls for systematic comparison
across data-generation frameworks. That is evidence for the lead, not proof
that any particular new benchmark would answer it. A smaller rerun of its
existing conditions would not be an adequate great-work contribution.

Ghaeli et al. compare six methods using real-data perturbations and synthetic
samples. Their limitations explicitly distinguish reproducibility from
biological correctness: a stable inferred network is not necessarily correct.
Hirano and Takemoto had already investigated ecological simulation versus
statistical assumptions in 2019; they excluded environmental perturbation and
identified it as a limitation. This is not a newly discovered concern.

Mustri et al. explain why a common target is difficult: the pairwise
Lotka–Volterra approximation can fail for consumer-resource dynamics when their
timescales are insufficiently separated. Their analysis includes persistence,
stability and reactivity, and warns about context-dependent pairwise inference.
Calling two different mathematical objects the same ground-truth edge would
make an apparently careful comparison misleading.

Opened sources:

- [Mariotto et al., 29 October 2026](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014898),
  scope, introduction and discussion of cross-framework ranking and preprocessing.
- [Ghaeli et al., 20 June 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-026-06510-3),
  methods, discussion, limitations and conclusion.
- [Hirano and Takemoto, 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-019-2915-1),
  ecological simulation and environmental-perturbation limitation.
- [Mustri et al., 3 December 2025](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013719),
  timescale-separation analysis and discussion of context-dependent interactions.

**Unresolved lead:** define a shared intervention or response target, distinguish
it from association and mechanistic coefficients, then identify an experiment
that adds to the current comparisons. This screen did not settle that design.
No need for a new account, spending or hardware was demonstrated; no resource
request is justified by this uncertainty alone.
