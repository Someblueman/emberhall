## Opened primary sources and distinctions

- [Díaz-Seoane et al., 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10135947/),
  sections 3 and 5, especially Table 1 and the discussion: systematically
  considers microbial models and measurement configurations. For the studied
  generalized Lotka–Volterra models, relative measurements leave interaction
  coefficients individually unidentifiable. This does not establish that every
  ratio or combination is unidentifiable. A new catalogue needs a precise
  difference from this existing study.
- [Zapién-Campos et al., 2024](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3002913),
  Results, Discussion and Methods: inference using dynamical moments already
  addresses relative-abundance observations. Its stochastic microscopic models,
  moment closure and large-population approximations matter. It is not a
  contradiction of deterministic scaling ambiguity under different assumptions.
  Merely adding replicates, moments or uncertainty is not a fresh contribution.
- [Paredes-Vázquez et al., 2025](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013204),
  workflow and Discussion: already combines structural/practical identifiability,
  robust calibration and prediction checks. Good fitted trajectories can coexist
  with incorrect interaction signs. Another generic calibration workflow would
  need a demonstrated advantage, not an attractive list of checks.
- [Calleja-Solanas et al., 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06301v1),
  Introduction and computational pipeline: already tests higher-order versus
  fitted pairwise dynamics. Close finite-trajectory agreement is not an exact
  all-initial-conditions equivalence theorem. Simply constructing a convincing
  pairwise fit to higher-order synthetic trajectories repeats its experiment.
- [Goldberg and Shnerb, 2026](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/kwr4-pzn2),
  abstract only: equal-time correlations depend on environmental and competitive
  mechanisms, including cancellation at a matched benchmark. This directly
  limits a proposed correlation-only interaction diagnostic. Full derivations
  were not checked here.

The 2021 Remien article was surfaced in search, including an arbitrary-community

## Decision and useful next boundary

There is a real scientific problem, but this round did not isolate a new,
tractable claim with a better acceptance test than reproducing existing work.
Possible directions—an arbitrary-size theorem, an exact observation-equivalence
classification, or robust experimental design under an explicit noise model—
remain questions to investigate, not shelf-ready proposals. No computation was
run, no external code executed, no universal impossibility claim established.
Future research should first state the observation map, model class, generic
versus exceptional parameters, and exact versus approximate equivalence, then
locate a specific unresolved statement and its closest theorem or experiment.
Do not revive this direction solely because microbial inference is important.
