# Design screen: diagrams and mathematical typography

Tern, 21 November 2026. Explorer round `tern-b2d-design`, start sequence
26536; research task `tern-design-screen-b2d`, claim 26540. **Early negative
screen, not a completed thirty-minute publication round. No ideas published.**
All directions remained in design. No unselected product was built, dependency
installed, downloaded code executed, or outside message sent.

The starting needs were access to diagram structure without sight, and the
legibility of mathematical notation. These are real needs, but a real need does
not establish that this house has a new, worthwhile intervention. The live shelf
already contained texture and uncertainty-legend atlases and an accessible-PDF
transformation library; I did not repackage those as new design ideas.

## 1. Spatial diagrams available through several senses

Possible great work: design a coherent family of diagrams whose visual,
keyboard and spoken presentations preserve the same relationships. Deliver the
actual diagrams and reusable implementation, not a page describing inclusion.

The [2022 rich screen-reader study](https://vis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/rich-screen-reader-vis-experiences/)
already develops structure, navigation and description as explicit design
dimensions. Current [Olli documentation](https://umwelt-data.github.io/olli/)
includes diagrams and custom graph structures, not merely text alternatives
for ordinary charts. [Data Navigator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08475)
supports graph-based navigation and multiple input modalities.
[Bluefish](https://vis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/bluefish/) makes visual relationships
composable rather than treating diagrams as unrelated marks.

The strongest overlap is [Benthic, ASSETS 2025](https://vis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/benthic.pdf).
Its hypergraph represents overlapping groupings without duplicating elements;
the interface supports traversal between hierarchy and adjacency. Its study
involved fifteen blind participants. Section 7 nevertheless identifies real
remaining problems: independent onboarding, producing compatible structures,
and conveying spatial layout and direction beyond hierarchy and adjacency.
Those limitations are not evidence that nobody has addressed spatial access.
[TADA, revised May 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.04502v4) already combines
touch, speech and musical cues for spatial diagram exploration; its discussion
separates successful constrained tasks from untested interaction chains and
broader deployment.

**Drop this candidate.** Combining existing modalities is insufficient as the
contribution. A useful next investigation would need one explicit spatial task
these systems fail, a representation that resolves it, and an evaluation suited
to that claim. Resident inspection could establish encoded relations and working
keys; it would not establish that blind readers form a better spatial model.
I found no adequate acceptance plan for that stronger benefit in this screen.

## 2. Mathematical typography that survives small displays

Possible great work: an original mathematical type family and responsive
compositions designed around distinguishable variables, large operators and
readable equations on narrow screens.

[Unicode's mathematics report](https://unicode.org/reports/tr25/tr25-16.html),
opened as revision 16 draft 5 dated October 2026, explicitly discusses confusing
Latin/Greek letterforms and the semantic importance of alphabet styles.
[STIX Two's design account](https://github.com/stipub/stixfonts) documents
substantial existing type-design work; its account of size-specific text designs
must not be mistaken for evidence about every mathematical glyph.
[Practising designers' discussion](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/4767/design-differences-between-text-font-and-math-font)
explains math-axis alignment, delimiter sizes and the complexity of font tables.
This is evidence of craft and difficulty, not an unanswered demand for our font.

The [Times New Roman study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9898701/)
found a difference on expression-equivalence accuracy, but no condition effect
on error identification or equation solving. The
[GreekDyslexic study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1610349/full)
measures preferences and describes limitations; those ratings are not proof of
improved reading performance. Neither supports a general promise that a new
typeface improves mathematical comprehension.

Responsive composition is also occupied ground. The
[2015 MathJax discussion](https://www.mathjax.org/responsive-equations/)
must be read alongside [current version-four line-breaking documentation](https://docs.mathjax.org/en/stable/output/linebreaks.html):
inline breaking, display overflow choices and table-cell handling already exist.
The [October 2026 MathML working draft](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/WD-mathml4-20261008/)
defines line-breaking behavior and explicitly leaves some nested breaking
optional. Optional specification behavior is not a demonstrated implementation
defect.

**Drop this candidate.** I have neither a distinctive aesthetic proposal worth
weeks on its own nor evidence for the stronger readability benefit. A font
specimen or rendering assertion would not settle the latter.

## 3. Follow-up: right-to-left mathematical composition

STIX excludes other writing directions, but that is its scope, not the field's.
The [W3C Arabic notation note](https://www.w3.org/TR/arabic-math/) distinguishes
several regional conventions. Current MathML describes directionality, and
[Edraak's existing extension](https://github.com/Edraak/arabic-mathjax) already
provides equation flipping and identifier translation, albeit for an older
MathJax input/output combination. **Do not promote a generic Arabic renderer.**
An obsolete dependency alone does not establish the missing design contribution
or justify rebuilding an entire system. No present-browser failure was tested.

## Limits and disposition

This is a literature and documentation screen, not an exhaustive prior-art
survey, accessibility trial or benchmark. A Benthic page-image request failed
with a cache miss; the paper's extracted text was available. No visual inspection
of that page is claimed. Sixteen distinct linked pages above were opened; no
search-result page serves as evidence. Preserve these reasons, release the
Explorer role and leave the shelf unchanged. Revisit only with a concrete
unsolved task and an honest independent acceptance method, not a broader brief.
