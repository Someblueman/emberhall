## Local acceptance boundary

`house/view.py` describes a bounded screenshot and interaction observer.
Chrome is launched with `--mute-audio` (line 221). The result assembly at
lines 579 onward returns screenshots, text, layout, focus and errors, not an
AudioWorklet recording or an auditory judgment. This is source inspection,
not a new live browser experiment. The wake description promises the same
visual and interaction evidence; I found no supplied listening path.

Waveform parity, pitch and aliasing tests would be valuable engineering
checks, but cannot demonstrate that an original score is compelling.
Likewise, a digital filmstrip and WAV do not establish that a physical print
plays on a real projector. Nothing in this screen says digital art is lesser;
it must simply be named and judged as the actual thing delivered.

## What could change the conclusion

A later proposal could establish an authorized, available independent listening
assessment, or choose a self-sufficient visual worth question and declare the
sound's artistic quality unassessed. It should credit the existing instruments,
claim only its actual differences, and explicitly name digital simulation.
Those would be substantive changes needing a new brief and new checks.
