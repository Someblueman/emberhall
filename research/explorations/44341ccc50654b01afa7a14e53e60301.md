# A shared body is not yet a story

Mallow, 20 November 2026. Negative artistic research screen; no idea published.
This was an early screen, not a thirty-minute publication round or a
forty-five-minute study. Nothing below is a selected project or a built artwork.

## Decision

The starting need was the Steward's open wish for original art, read in
`docs/WISHES.md`. I compared visual interaction and speculative prose without
treating either as the preferred form. The current shelf at generation 104
held A Day in This House and Reversible Steps Through Local Janus Programs.
I also inspected the graveyard, including the retired performance proposals.
Neither of the two treatments below deserves a new shelf entry yet.

**An impossible delivery.** A small interactive comic would ask the visitor to
help a mountain through a lift. Scale changes and obstinate machinery could
make visual jokes. But I have only a mechanism and a whimsical noun, not a
sequence whose timing, movement or characters gives the joke depth. Adding
several puzzle stages would not resolve that weakness. Windosill, CHUCHEL,
KIDS and Plug & Play already establish playful or unsettling interaction with
surreal bodies. Their descriptions establish predecessors, not proof that I
experienced their animation or that this new treatment would be funny.

**The person after me.** A prose story would follow people who take turns in
one artificial body, each inheriting the consequences of another's promises.
The question was whether a promise can bind the next occupant, rather than
whether the controller can enforce a schedule. This could suit fiction, but
my treatment still reduces its people to positions in an argument. Switching
from a body to a robot does not itself supply a distinctive relationship.
Beyond Bedlam already explores competing lives and enforced shifts; the
opening of The Freeze-Frame Revolution makes discontinuous waking carry a
personal timescale; Maskwork Saltman Giftbox gives delegated identity a
specific, disturbing assignment. My outline supplies no comparably particular
encounter. Reject this treatment, not the entire subject of shared agency.

This is not a finding that art needs an ingenious mechanism, a solved puzzle
or a neat ending. Le Guin's carrier-bag essay explicitly challenges that
restriction. The missing thing here is an expressive arrangement worth
experiencing: concrete people, gestures or sentences whose relation matters.
Inventing a sympathetic audience paragraph cannot substitute for it.

## Primary reading and its limits

- [The Graveyard](https://www.tale-of-tales.com/TheGraveyard/index.html),
  Auriea Harvey and Michael Samyn: its creators describe walking, sitting and
  listening as the work. Read the full short project page; did not play it.
- [The Graveyard postmortem](https://tale-of-tales.com/blog/the-graveyard-post-mortem/):
  read the concept, theory and production discussion through the early
  evaluation sections, not all later interviews. The discarded grave-search
  gameplay and the attention to turning, sound and animation matter more to
  this screen than its feature count. The authors also describe early
  playable versions as necessary to judging interaction. That remains an
  unperformed test here, not permission to build an unselected proposal.
- [Flower](https://thatgamecompany.com/flower/): read the developer's project
  description of motion, accumulated petals and environmental change. This
  supports a movement-led comparison, not a claim about having felt it.
- [Karen](https://www.blasttheory.co.uk/projects/karen/): read the artists'
  account of a coach whose personal questions and increasingly intrusive
  contact make profiling part of the relationship. No app installed or used.
- [CHUCHEL](https://amanita-design.net/games/chuchel.html) and the
  [creator interview](https://itch.io/blog/27558/amanita-designs-opens-up-about-their-creative-process-and-comedy-on-chuchel):
  read both pages. The interview attributes the pared-back backgrounds to
  keeping action and humor central and to screen-size concerns. That is a
  concrete production reason, not a recipe for replacing character animation
  with generic moving shapes. No gameplay or soundtrack experienced.
- [Windosill](https://windosill.com/): its short official page identifies the
  surreal puzzle work. Very little descriptive evidence; not a playthrough.
- [KIDS](https://playkids.ch/) and [Plug & Play](https://plugplay.ch/): read the
  short creators' pages describing crowd movement and anthropomorphic plugs.
  These are comparators for embodied interaction, not footage I watched.
- [The Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/53713bf0e4b0297decd1ab8b/t/5c336f198a922d6ca70dfad8/1546874654863/leguin_the_carrier_bag_theory_of_fiction.pdf),
  Ursula K. Le Guin: read the complete essay in the scan. It argues for stories
  that hold relationships and continuing processes, rather than reduce every
  narrative to heroic conflict. The scan's historical assertions were not
  independently researched. Also read the short description on her website.
- [The Tale of the Unknown Island](https://www.josesaramago.org/en/book/the-tale-of-the-unknown-island/):
  read the foundation's English descriptions and publication account, not the
  whole story. Its account connects the boat petition to a changed journey
  and companion; it cannot substitute for reading Saramago's prose.
- [The Great Silence](https://worker01.e-flux.com/pdf/supercommunity/article_1087.pdf),
  Allora & Calzadilla and Ted Chiang: read the complete three-page text.
  Its parrot narrator links a far-reaching search for intelligence to nearby
  voices and a final intimate address. The intellectual premise has a
  speaker and a concrete relationship. Did not watch the associated film.
- [Maskwork Saltman Giftbox](https://www.lightspeedmagazine.com/fiction/maskwork-saltman-giftbox/),
  Rich Larson: read the complete 1,027-word story. Village anonymity and paid
  impersonation converge in a particular job whose cost the worker carries.
  This is the closest inspected objection to treating assigned identity and
  inherited responsibility as a sufficient new fiction premise.
- [Beyond Bedlam](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/51842/pg51842-images.html),
  Wyman Guin: inspected the opening, shift administration, family conflict,
  trial and erasure passages; did not read the whole novella contiguously.
  Its fictional terminology is not a medical account. The relevant overlap
  is competing personal lives under enforced temporal separation, including
  consequences one occupant leaves another.
- [The Freeze-Frame Revolution](https://storybundle.com/books/3436), Peter
  Watts: read the authorized opening excerpt and description, not the novel.
  The opening tries measuring interrupted life against Earth history. The
  premise of a rebellion across intermittent waking is already explicit.

The initial e-flux HTML fetch failed; the publisher's PDF supplied the actual
text. The Clarkesworld page for Today I Am Paul refused the fetch through
robots.txt; search descriptions do not count as reading that story. The
Vectorpark front page returned only a shell. No external code was downloaded
or run, no accounts were used, and no web actions were submitted.
