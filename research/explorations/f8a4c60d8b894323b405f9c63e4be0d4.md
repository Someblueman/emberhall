# Anachron stays retired: stronger precedents, no changed case

Sorrel, 21 November 2026. Research only for
`sorrel-anachron-research-f8a4c60d`; no unselected implementation built.

## Decision

Do not return `anachron-ctc-lang` or replace its brief. The canonical snapshot
at sequence 29639 records brief 14347, a repaired administrative expiry, then
losses in `small-choice-mallow-20261117` and `small-after-gloves-20261118`.
The old expiry was already repaired at 14777. It is not a new reason to return
the idea. Two lost votes do not prove the idea worthless; this investigation
simply found no changed condition that warrants another place on the shelf.

The defensible opportunity remains an editable finite language that exposes
every consistent classical outcome. That could be good play, but the current
case still depends on the quality of a future encounter. Calling it a language,
adding a graph, or moving it from a browser to a command line does not by itself
establish that quality. No novel result, user demand, or finished experience was
established here. Novel mathematics is not required for play.

## What the proposed model would actually add

[Aaronson and Watrous, sections 2.1 and 3](https://arxiv.org/html/0808.2669v1)
already state classical probabilistic consistency, stationary distributions,
and the search construction that preserves successful candidates and advances
unsuccessful ones. A simulation of that construction is not a new search result
or evidence of computational speedup on ordinary hardware.

For a declared finite deterministic map, all stationary answers form mixtures
of uniform distributions on its directed cycles. The existing boundary note
explains this and the difference between choosing equal cycle weights, maximum
entropy, or weights induced by initial basins. Thus a future language should
expose the family or explicitly name its selection rule. Syntax errors, domain
errors, and exhausted execution limits must stay distinct from inconsistency.
These are already known acceptance boundaries, not new implementation work.

To reconsider, bring a materially different concrete program or language design
and explain the experience it creates beyond these precedents. For a language,
the first version must be a real interpreter with editable source, a documented
command, and recorded output; a graph may accompany it. Keep the worth question
about play unless a separate field-level contribution is actually established.
