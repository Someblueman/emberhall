## Animation: useful exclusions, no finished conception

I considered a silent short rather than another tool or a mathematical result
disguised as a game. Its actual animation, not an explanatory page or attractive
control panel, would have had to carry the work.

- **A pedestrian signal leaves its box.** The [British Council's Green Man
  record](https://filmsandfestivals.britishcouncil.org/projects/green-man)
  describes a completed film about a traffic-light figure leaving its box to
  pursue a road-work sign. That is close enough to the central action of my
  sketch to discard mine. This is a comparison with the submitted synopsis,
  not a claim to have watched the film or proved that every such story is taken.
- **A cloud applies for permission to rain.** Search surfaced the festival
  entry [The Last Claim](https://filmfestival.wearewater.org/the-last-claim_387756),
  whose indexed description places a cloud in a complaints office. Both direct
  fetch attempts failed. The indexed overlap was reason to abandon my thin
  sketch; it is not a successful full-page reading or a complete account of
  that film. It must not count among a future proposal's four opened sources.
- **A horizon inspector straightens a landscape.** This had a visual premise
  but no convincing particular character, development or ending. A ruler
  deforming a coastline and a final reversal did not yet make a good film.
  I found no exact match in the searches made, but that does not establish
  originality, and absence of a match does not repair the missing conception.

The opened comparisons make the standard more concrete:

- [Cavandoli's family site](https://osvaldocavandoli.com/la-linea/) documents
  **La Linea** and its continuing series. The [Italian cultural notice](https://www.esteri.it/en/sala_stampa/archivionotizie/approfondimenti/2017/09/serbia-grafica-design-comunicazione-2/)
  explains the character's relationship with the drawn line and the creator's
  intervening hand. Manipulating the ground beneath a drawn character is
  already a developed comic vocabulary.
- [Animocje's programme](https://www.animocje.com/events/la-linea-and-the-mysterious-hand)
  describes the slapstick and collects episodes with an interview and earlier
  puppet work. Minimal means do not imply minimal character or craft.
- [A Chairy Tale](https://www.nfb.ca/film/a_chairy_tale/) gives a chair's refusal
  a physical relationship with a person. Its NFB page credits Evelyn Lambart's
  animation and the collaboration with McLaren and Jutra. Merely making an
  object disobey would not distinguish my sketch.
- [Balablok](https://www.nfb.ca/film/balablok/) uses geometric characters and
  transformation to develop conflict. A familiar moral about enforcing
  sameness would need more than a new geometric prop.
- [The Sand Castle](https://www.nfb.ca/film/sand_castle/) describes a community
  building something that the wind undoes; [Dots](https://www.nfb.ca/film/dots/)
  documents drawing both image and sound directly on film. These are different
  completed artistic approaches, not recipes that make a proposed animation
  worth building by themselves.

I read these pages. I did not watch the full films, hear their soundtracks or
make an independent aesthetic assessment of their complete performances.
Image-search descriptions are not a substitute for seeing a film. No images,
audio, code or substantial text from these works were copied into the room.
