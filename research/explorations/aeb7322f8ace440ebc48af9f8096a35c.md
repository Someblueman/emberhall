over the other fields. The current shelf and its graveyard were read before

## Every Promise Has Two Endings

A substantial playable novella follows an interpreter helping two towns
reopen a bridge after a flood. People agree on words and disagree about
what carrying them out requires. The proposed interpreter has her own
loyalties and limits; neither town is a single personality. The appeal must
come from people the reader wants to meet again, not a hidden correct clause.

[First Draft's author statement](https://lizadaly.com/first-draft/) treats
revision as characterization. Its [release discussion](https://emshort.blog/2012/09/24/first-draft-of-the-revolution-released/)
includes a reader expecting different later outcomes and the author's
explanation of why she chose a largely fixed arc. The
[implementation account](https://www.inklestudios.com/2012/09/24/first-draft-of-the-revolution)
also distinguishes the browser and ebook versions. This supports choosing
one playable format, not promising universal ebook compatibility.
I also read the opening letters and their alternatives in the
[published story data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lizadaly/first-draft-of-the-revolution/master/OEBPS/story.js).
The alternatives reveal a character's intentions and inhibitions; this is
more specific than exchanging polite and angry synonyms. The file was read
as text, never executed.

[Conversation as Gameplay](https://emshort.blog/2019/01/20/conversation-as-gameplay-talk/)
sets the harder standard: understandable agency with dramatic flow.
[Galatea's proposed Versu remake](https://emshort.blog/2016/02/23/the-versu-galatea/)
already explored remembered dialogue, changed viewpoint and expanded endings.
[Polaris](https://www.tao-games.com/polaris/), also discussed in
[Short's play account](https://emshort.blog/2013/05/03/tabletop-storygames-polaris/),
already negotiates narrative consequences. These rule out claiming a new
mechanic. The [Heaven's Vault interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/road-to-the-igf-inkle-s-i-heaven-s-vault-i-)
and [its language-design account](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-inkle-developed-its-own-ancient-language-for-i-heaven-s-vault-i-)
likewise rule out claiming that uncertain interpretation driving story is new.

[Sojourner's design account](https://www.echodoggames.com/blog/2019/04/19/what-s-a-narrative-card-game-anyway/)
warns that mechanics can overwhelm relationships. [Chants of Sennaar](https://www.rundisc.io/chants-of-sennaar/)
and [Xenolanguage](https://thornygames.com/pages/xenolanguage-a-game-about-alien-language-and-human-memory)
are further close comparisons. The proposal survives only as its particular
authored story. Gate: a complete chapter and next morning, with consequential
choices; an independent reader cares about someone and voluntarily revisits
an exchange. Arbitrary punishment, caricature or mere answer-hunting fails.

## Breakfast Falls Sideways

An eight-to-ten-minute wordless animated comedy puts one waiter and three
guests under different gravities. Its form is a finished film with original
drawing, timing and music, exported as video. The initial piano-delivery
premise was abandoned after reading the
[Music Box production essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/music_box.pdf).
[One Week](https://busterkeaton.org/the-films/one-week/) supplies another
close architectural comedy, not permission to repeat its scenes.

[Relativity](https://www.escherinhetpaleis.nl/en/about-escher/masterpieces/relativity?lang=en)
and [Escher's gravity discussion](https://www.escherinhetpaleis.nl/en/about-escher/escher-today/gravity)
establish the image's ancestry. [William Chyr's development account](https://blog.playstation.com/2015/09/16/cultivate-an-infinite-world-in-manifold-garden/)
already moves beyond gravity switching to consequences. Most decisively,
[Balance's makers](https://www.lauenstein-brothers.com/balance/) describe
people interacting on a suspended platform. The [NFB's Neighbours account](https://blog.nfb.ca/blog/2011/02/27/neighbours-the-nfbs-second-oscar-winner/)
also establishes wordless social conflict through physical action.

Thus the claim is neither new geometry nor the discovery of social physics.
It is a particular comedy of appetite, service and dignity. Gate: one finished
60-to-90-second scene watched without explanation. The reviewer can identify
the waiter's goal, the reversal and a gesture worth replaying. Sliding diagram
figures, a necessary physics caption or camera rotation as the sole pleasure
fails before full production. A longer film alone is not a better film.
