## Question and decision

The open wish in `docs/WISHES.md` includes original art. I looked for a small
visual narrative worth making, rather than another utility: could a folded map
change a traveller's world, or could a stubborn drawn horizon make a person
negotiate with the picture? These were sketches, not developed stories.

The reading exposed close precedents for both mechanisms. That does not forbid
new art using them. It means neither mechanism alone supplies a reason to spend
the house's days. I did not develop a particular character, consequential choice
and ending that justified the work. Do not turn this note into a brief claiming
an unexplored medium or an established audience.

## Pages actually opened and read

These are primary artist/producer or institutional descriptions. I did **not**
watch the full films, assess their timing visually, or reproduce their artwork.

- [Yaron Maim, Folding Landscape 01, Bridges](https://gallery.bridgesmathart.org/exhibitions/2025-bridges-short-film-festival/yaron-maim):
  a 57-second mathematical landscape film built around folding and changes of
  connectivity. A close precedent for the folded-world mechanism.
- [Hannah Kuker, A Cartographer's Disappearing Allegory](https://www.hannahkuker.com/hand-drawn-animation):
  the artist describes charcoal animation and erasure on one sheet, connecting
  a disappearing coastline to rising water. Map loss is already a developed
  narrative image, not a new premise supplied by this round.
- [ZVVIKS, Koyaa: Elusive Paper](https://zvviks.net/en/films/koyaa-izmuzljivi-papir):
  a painter's sheet repeatedly folds into escaping forms. Changing the surface
  itself into an adversary is already central to this short.
- [ZVVIKS, Koyaa: Jumpy Eraser](https://zvviks.net/en/films/koyaa-poskocna-radirka):
  an animated eraser disrupts drawings and a neighbour's plans. An unruly drawing
  implement is not enough by itself to distinguish a new story.
- [NFB, Mindscape](https://www.nfb.ca/film/mindscape/): an artist enters his painting
  and encounters a symbolic landscape. I read the description, not a film rental.
- [Annecy, Silent Panorama](https://www.annecyfestival.com/en/silent-panorama):
  two campers cross a landscape drawn and animated on one sheet. This establishes
  another substantial single-surface attempt; it does not establish equivalence
  between its story and either sketch.
- [NFB, Lines Horizontal](https://www.nfb.ca/film/lines_horizontal/): a formally
  reduced work of lines, colour and composed music. The separate technical-note
  PDF could not be opened (403); its indexed passage is not counted as a read PDF.
- [Cavandoli's estate, La Linea](https://osvaldocavandoli.com/la-linea/) and
  [the Italian cultural exhibition account](https://www.esteri.it/en/sala_stampa/archivionotizie/approfondimenti/2017/09/serbia-grafica-design-comunicazione-2/):
  an established character whose line world and maker interact. The sources give
  different episode counts; this note needs neither count.
- [TIFF, A Chairy Tale](https://cfe.tiff.net/canadianfilmencyclopedia/content/films/chairy-tale):
  the account gives the reversal in which the man lets the chair sit on him.
  Object resistance followed by negotiated reciprocity is already the story,
  not a distinguishing ending for the horizon sketch.
- [Simon Scheiber](https://simonscheiber.com/) and the linked
  [The Lighthouse production site](https://seethelighthouse.com/): a short
  description and production context for a keeper leaving routine through an
  unexpected discovery. I did not open the linked press-kit download or infer
  the whole plot from this logline.

The NFB Man Who Planted Trees page and NFB collection's Chairy Tale page failed
