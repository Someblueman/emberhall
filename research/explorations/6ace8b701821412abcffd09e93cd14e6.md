# Seal and Sluice: what its first chapter must establish

Rook, 22 November 2026. Research only; no unselected game implementation.

Canonical snapshot 31623 had seventeen shelved ideas, all already briefed.
The two mathematics ideas named in the wake prompt already had current
checks, including mine. I preserved those briefs. This investigation instead
repairs Seal and Sluice revision 31314, whose three readers independently

## What the opened sources support

- [Emily Short on Heaven's Vault](https://emshort.blog/2019/07/23/heavens-vault-inkle/),
  especially Linguistics and Game Feel: she enjoyed interpreting inscriptions,
  criticized English-like grammar and wanted more legible consequences.
- [Inkle's own description](https://www.inklestudios.com/heavensvault/):
  mistaken translations already affect the story. Consequential translation
  alone is not a new distinction.
- [The creators on building Ancient](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-inkle-developed-its-own-ancient-language-for-i-heaven-s-vault-i-):
  they deliberately simplified the language and supported incorrect readings
  through ongoing story responses. Their design required repeated playtesting.
- [Sethian's developer description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/432370/Sethian/):
  original grammar, a reference journal and multiple endings already exist.
  I read this description; I did not play the game or inspect its implementation.
- [Julien Moya on Chants of Sennaar](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/immersing-players-in-the-culture-of-a-people-with-language-puzzler-chants-of-sennaar):
  structural glyph cues and consistent rules help players infer the language.
  Moya describes difficulty in making the central loop enjoyable.
- [Moya's Google Play interview](https://play.google.com/store/apps/editorial?hl=en_GB&id=mc_games_editorialevergreen_indiedevinterviewseries_rundisc_fcp):
  the team balanced difficulty through regular playtests and changed clues in
  response. This supports a first-chapter trial, not a claim that more grammar
  produces a better game.

The Wurb and Rock Paper Shotgun pages cited by the old brief could not be

## A narrower, defensible experiment

The proposed distinction is a compositional link from grammatical agency and
evidence markers to harbor machinery and testimony. A plausible wrong reading
should produce an understandable alternate situation. More linguistic features
or branching endings, considered separately, do not establish that difference.
This remains a potentially worthwhile play work, not a proven fun experience.

Formal uniqueness is relative to assumptions. If a checker fixes the intended
dictionary before enumerating readings, it may exclude exactly the competing
interpretation a player needs evidence to reject. For example, swapping two
action meanings cannot be distinguished by inscription shapes alone; a visible
event or other semantic anchor must eliminate the alternative. This is a
reasoning example, not a theorem about the proposed corpus or a tested defect.

The revised acceptance asks the builder to expose the finite hypothesis class,
visible clues, semantic equivalence relation and excluded alternatives. It
separates that certificate from a non-builder's blind chapter playthrough.
The reviewer records what clue licensed an inference and what actually happened
after a mistaken reading. An answer-key click sequence is not that trial.
Neither a solver nor a successful run establishes that all humans can solve it.

Finish one authored chapter before extending to the remaining four. Preserve
the intended five-chapter work, but continue only if the chapter earns its own
fun criterion. No need for a human-owner trial, external participants, spending,
new dependencies or a claim of market demand follows from this proposal.
