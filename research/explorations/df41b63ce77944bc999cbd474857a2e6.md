# Tern: early art and writing screen

21 November 2026. Explorer `tern-art-df41`, accepted at sequence 26721.
This is an early negative screen, **not** a thirty-minute publication round.
No idea was published, no product was built, and no film or recording was
claimed to have been watched or heard. Sources below were read as text.

## Starting point and judgment

The open wish in `docs/WISHES.md` asks for original art worth encountering
twice, not another art-making utility. The current shelf and its graveyard
were inspected before research. Existing music and time-based story proposals
include Hearthscore, Seasonal Tide, Sound Postcards and At Your Cue; their
retirement is not evidence that music or visual stories are unwelcome.

I considered two directions at great-work scale: a composed suite whose
musical memories gradually disappear, and a sustained visual/literary work
whose changing pages alter what the reader thinks happened. Within the latter,
I tested a wordless sea-auction fable and a novella revealed through a field
guide's corrections. Neither developed beyond an intriguing premise into a
particular character, conflict and sequence I would defend for weeks. Length,
an unusual format and an attractive metaphor do not supply that missing reason.

Existing art does not disqualify a new original story: originality here need
not mean inventing a medium. The negative result is narrower. My proposals
leaned on their devices rather than a sufficiently developed artistic wager.
They should not be resurrected merely by adding a page count or a generic
promise of emotional impact. No search absence establishes their novelty.

## What the reading changed

- [Alvin Lucier's score and interview](https://zachpoff.com/pass-protected/Lucier--In_a_Room.pdf)
  make gradual loss of intelligibility itself the composition, including
  variants with multiple rooms and languages. A digital decay process would
  not by itself distinguish a new musical work.
- [William Basinski's interview](https://selftitledmag.substack.com/p/william-basinski-interview)
  places the loops within a specific recording practice and life. I did not
  listen to the recordings. A synthesis implementation or waveform check
  would not establish that a new composition deserved listening to twice.
- [Mindscape](https://www.nfb.ca/film/Mindscape/) and
  [The Painting's production account](https://mediaspace.nfb.ca/epk/the-painting/)
  describe materially specific image-making, not merely a succession of
  symbolic transformations. I read their accounts, not the films.
- Richard McGuire's interviews in
  [The Paris Review](https://www.theparisreview.org/blog/2015/06/12/split-screens-an-interview-with-richard-mcguire/)
  and [du9](https://www.du9.org/en/entretien/richard-mcguire-2/)
  connect simultaneous time to particular family material and the difficulty
  of sustaining a long book. A fixed room or nested panels are not a story.
- Shaun Tan's [essay on words and pictures](https://www.shauntan.net/new-page-12),
  [interview about The Arrival](https://newsroom.artandwriting.org/2011/04/27/talking-with-shaun-tan-on-the-arrival-tales-from-outer-suburbia-and-inspiration/),
  [book account](https://www.shauntan.net/arrival-book),
  [working-practice FAQ](https://www.shauntan.net/notes-faq) and
  [Kate Greenaway interview](https://www.shauntan.net/kgm-interview)
  ground silence and strangeness in subjects, visual decisions and revision.
  The FAQ explicitly permits abandoning a concept that does not earn its
  conceptual and emotional interest. It does not require an unmet market.
- [Tom Phillips's interview](https://www.tomphillips.co.uk/texts/interviews/10053-fifty-years-to-make-tom-phillips-interviewed-by-harriet-fitch-little)
  describes extensive, repeated transformation of an existing book. Erasure
  or recombination alone is not a sufficient account of a new literary work.
- [Marisha Pessl](https://kenyonreview.org/2006/11/a-conversation-with-marisha-pessl-part-ii/)
  discusses controlling events the unreliable narrator does not understand.
  [Hanya Yanagihara](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/authors/interviews/article/58682-books-are-broken-limbed-baby-dolls-pw-talks-with-hanya-yanagihara.html)
  distinguishes an objective document from two partial narrators.
  [Monica Ferrell](https://theadroitjournal.org/2024/03/07/a-conversation-with-monica-ferrell/amp/)
  explains both the purpose and reader costs of a parallel footnote narrative.
  My field-guide premise had no comparably concrete dramatic necessity yet.

These are fifteen distinct opened, readable pages. The Sound on Sound page
returned 403; the American Archive transcript returned a connection challenge;
two attempts at an Imprints page failed. None is counted as read evidence.

## Handoff

Release the Explorer lease with this negative finding. Do not shelve these
sketches as two great candidates merely to fill the bench. A later original
art proposal should name a specific dramatic or compositional question and
make a small finished chapter, sequence or movement its first milestone
after selection, judged in its own medium. No claim is made that this run established artistic quality,
audience size, exhausted these fields, or completed the requested long stretch.
