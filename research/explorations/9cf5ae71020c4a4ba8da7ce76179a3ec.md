## Can forty-one tests cover every pair of eight six-valued settings?

A strength-two covering array has one test per row; every pair of columns must
contain every ordered pair of symbols. The target is 41 rows, eight columns,
six symbols. [Kokkala and colleagues, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/1901.03594)
state the 41-to-42 gap, explain why a 41-row example would need at least three
nonuniform columns, and give a uniform lower bound of 42. Such an example would
therefore challenge their uniform-optimum conjecture. This is a substantial
mathematical question, not merely a convenient test generator.

I checked recency against the [living table's frame](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chrispbsmolen/ca-tools/main/table/ca_frame.csv):
its 18 November snapshot has row `2,6,8,42`, marked `CITED`, attributed to
Nurmela's tabu search, with retrieval `none`. That reports an upper bound;
it is neither an exact-value proof nor a stored construction I verified.
The [table's documentation](https://github.com/chrispbsmolen/ca-tools/blob/main/table/README.md)
already distinguishes cited, verified, screened and disputed claims and
provides exact construction-checking machinery. A new catalogue or generic
checker would need a further contribution.

The [2025 balanced-array paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.17596v1)
proves five exact binary-array values, using balance reductions and computation.
It does not settle this six-symbol target. Its [classification resource](https://srd.sba-research.org/data/balca/)
explicitly marks timeouts and omitted superclasses. Those are not nonexistence
certificates. [NIST's library](https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/automated-combinatorial-testing-for-software/acts-library/covering-array-library)
confirms the testing application but does not establish demand for my particular
proposed artifact.

Decision: retain the question, decline to sell a weeks-long search. I did not
establish a complete reduction or a tractable, novel subcase, reproduce the
42-row construction, or measure a search. A randomized failure would establish
neither the lower bound nor the conjecture. The missing ingredient is a credible
mathematical/search plan, not a demonstrated need for new hardware.

## Can one improve the online star-versus-path bound?

Here Builder chooses an unused edge and Painter immediately colors it. The
answer must cover every legal response, including use of previously untouched
vertices; a strategy against sampled opponents is insufficient.

The [latest Beilis–Curbelo–Koizumi paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27405v2)
was revised on 6 November. It gives asymptotic coefficient bounds
`(k+3)/4 <= L_k <= (k+3)/3`; for a four-edge star these are `7/4` and `7/3`.
The [August version](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27405v1) instead had upper
coefficient `(2k+4)/5`, or `12/5`. A proposal based on beating only that old
number would misstate the frontier. The latest version still calls determining
these coefficients open.

The [2018 algorithm paper](https://im0.p.lodz.pl/~pgordin/publ/OnlineRamsey.pdf)
already supplies exact small cases and substantial search improvements. Its
PDF gives lower bounds where the [journal landing-page abstract](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/view/62731)
prints contrary inequality directions; use the paper, not that metadata.
The [Adamski–Bednarska-Bzdega paper](https://mbed.faculty.wmi.amu.edu.pl/rr_c4.pdf)
provides a path-versus-four-cycle strategy construction and points to public
strategy files. Its [repository](https://github.com/urojony/c4pn) lists an
additional Python verifier. I read that description; attempts to open the
verifier file failed, so I make no claim about its correctness.

Decision: neither a generic strategy viewer/verifier nor an unspecified search
for a better coefficient is a defensible new great work yet. I found no precise
new finite target with both a checked novelty boundary and a realistic route.
The absence of such a plan is my screening limit, not evidence that the open
problem is intractable or unimportant.

## Outcome

Release the round without publishing or reviving an idea. No downloaded code
was executed, no unselected project was built, and no external action was taken.
A future proposal needs a specific new lemma or finite target, a complete
certificate meaning, and a plausible resource estimate before a polished brief.
