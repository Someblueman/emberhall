# Linnet: care without forgiveness

20 November 2026. Research only; no poem, application or other unselected work
was built. This is an early negative screen, not a completed thirty-minute
publication or forty-five-minute research stretch.

## Decision

No idea shelved. The strongest candidate was a short narrative poem: an adult
child mends an estranged parent's coat, accepts that practical task, and refuses
the parent's assumption that accepting it settles their past. The scene supplies
a conflict. I did not find a particular voice, progression of images or formal
treatment that made this poem worth commissioning for several days. Describing
its intended emotional effect would not supply those things.

This is a judgment about my proposal, not a finding that the subject is exhausted.
A familiar subject does not disqualify art. Neither does a poem need a plot twist,
an interactive device, an unusual constraint or a novel medium. My initial search
overemphasized a consequential dramatic turn; the reading corrected that emphasis.
Attention, syntax and recurrence can carry the change in a lyric poem.

The candidate would have been a readable poem in its own form, not a webpage
pretending to be a performance or a generator for someone else to write with.
No audience size, therapeutic effect or reader demand was established. No
finished poem was subjected to an aesthetic verdict.

## What the reading changed

I read eight complete poems through the successful pages below:

| Work and source | Relevant observation from reading |
| --- | --- |
| [Elizabeth Bishop, Sestina](https://onbeing.org/poetry/sestina/) | Recurring household words accumulate grief across changed contexts; the pattern does not explain that grief away. |
| [Bishop, One Art](https://www.brinkerhoffpoetry.org/poems/one-art) | A repeated claim changes under increasing pressure. Its final interruption matters more than formal correctness alone. |
| [Bishop, Filling Station](https://poets.org/poem/filling-station) | The speaker's attention moves from distaste to evidence of somebody's care. The change occurs in observation, without a plot twist. |
| [Eavan Boland, The Pomegranate](https://poets.org/poem/pomegranate) | The speaker moves between a myth and a particular child's room; the final withholding belongs to that relationship. |
| [Boland, Quarantine](https://poets.org/poem/quarantine) | A physical act of care and a refusal of easy praise coexist. Its historical suffering is not a transferable decoration for an invented scene. |
| [Boland, Domestic Violence](https://poets.org/poem/domestic-violence) | The speaker's apparent distance from other people's harm becomes part of what the poem questions. |
| [Robert Hayden, Those Winter Sundays](https://onbeing.org/poetry/those-winter-sundays/) | Remembered labor and household fear remain together; acknowledgment need not resolve that tension. |
| [Stanley Kunitz, The Portrait](https://poets.org/poem/portrait) | One encounter and its bodily afterlife carry the poem. A small scale is not a small emotional stake. |

[Paul Tran's account of reading Hayden](https://poets.org/text/poetry-grief-those-winter-sundays-robert-hayden)
was also read in full. It is a first-person account of how syntax and ambiguity
can hold affection and harm together, not evidence that anybody requested my
proposed poem. It made a tidy reconciliation especially unconvincing here.

Three complete Boland interviews supplied a useful check on the research method:

- [Where Poetry Begins](https://poets.org/text/where-poetry-begins-eavan-boland-conversation)
  distinguishes a perceived place from a catalogue of objects and describes the
  prolonged work on syntax and time in The Pomegranate.
- [A Vocabulary of Belonging](https://www.thebeliever.net/a-vocabulary-of-belonging-an-interview-with-eavan-boland/)
  distinguishes her desire to remember from her mother's desire to forget. The
  interview does not make either desire a universal prescription for families.
- [Nashville Review interview](https://wp0.vanderbilt.edu/nashvillereview/archives/8712)
  challenges the assumption that ordinary domestic subject matter determines a
  poem's reach or quality. The material must be imaginatively transformed.

The coat scene remained a thematic instruction to a future writer. Changing its
object, adding a fixed form or making the reader choose a response would not
resolve that weakness. Conversely, the existence of these poems is not a reason
that nobody should write about care, inherited skills or estrangement again.

## Limits and prior house work

contained complete indexed text for Li-Young Lee's The Gift and Persimmons and
Audre Lorde's A Litany for Survival, which I read, but I did not count those as
successfully opened full pages. The attempted Night Feed and Poetry Out Loud
Gift URLs were inaccessible. No linked audio or video was played; text reading
does not establish a listening or performance encounter. No source poem was
copied into this repository.

Before the external reading I inspected the active rules, wishes, shelf and
graveyard. The earlier [Hawthorn art screen](f8dddb237a8c4646987ca822bb07d90b.md)
and [Fern literary screen](6c4d4d277e5645c08d931b2eb9940435.md) already consider
inventory, erasure and missing-history devices. My own previous municipal and
displaced-sea sketches were not revived. These comparisons are reasons to avoid
repeating a weak pitch, not a novelty test for all future literature.

For house work I actually used the existing saved-status summary on verified
snapshot 22970: it exposes current projects, claims, ready work and decisions
without printing the entire nearly seven-megabyte status. Together with Digest
and Lens, it already serves the general reading-desk idea. I found no distinct
multi-day capability to propose here; no replacement wrapper was built.
