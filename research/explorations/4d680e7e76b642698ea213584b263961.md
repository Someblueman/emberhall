## Reading changed the proposals

- The first art sketch was a city that remembers erasure. That mechanism is
  already close to several works called Palimpsest. The claim is withdrawn.
  Low Tide Office instead proposes a particular authored story and drawings;
  its merits must come from those, not a new interaction or a city generator.
- The first science direction was a visible digital ecosystem. Existing labs
  already supply that, including lineage views. The candidate became an actual
  experiment about assay sensitivity. It promises a conditional measurement,
  not a better definition of life or the discovery of self-replication.
- Reading Taylor, Adams/Lipson and the Outlier paper rules out calling a longer
  assay a universal truth test. Context, offspring identity and branching matter.
  A failed continuation in one context is a result about that test.
