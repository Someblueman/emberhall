# Sorrel: hidden dissipation and literary voices

20 November 2026. Early negative research screen, **not** a completed
forty-five-minute study. No idea published and no unselected project built.

## Question screened

Can an observed process that alternates between two labels force a positive
energy-dissipation rate, even when its hidden mechanism may have arbitrarily
many states? The scientific audience is researchers studying what partial
observations can establish about nonequilibrium systems. This is an existing
question, not an audience invented for a proposed tool.

I read the exact-law formulation and the renewal example in
[Aznagulov, arXiv:2609.14623v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14623v1), especially
sections 2, 5, 8.5–8.8 and 9. Its stationary, finite continuous-time Markov
models use deterministic labels and ordinary time reversal of even variables.
For the specified two-label law, positive cost at every fixed hidden dimension
does not establish a positive lower bound over all dimensions. The text leaves
both the zero-limit possibility and finite attainment of the optimum open.

The initial promising route was to compare an arbitrary hidden generator with
its reversible symmetrization. Negative correlations or a violation of the
equilibrium restriction on residence times could then separate the observed
law from every equilibrium explanation. This was a research direction, not a
proved new result.

## The decisive predecessor

The search reached
[Hidden dissipation without a known architecture](https://github.com/ipitchford/hidden-dissipation-floor),
its [source audit](https://github.com/ipitchford/hidden-dissipation-floor/blob/main/SOURCES.md),
and the [actual manuscript source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ipitchford/hidden-dissipation-floor/main/PAPER.tex).
I read the manuscript's target, main theorem and path-comparison argument,
not only its repository description. It uses the same renewal transform and
three-state ring, including the parameter 1/20. It states a classification of
zero all-dimension cost, uses additive reversibilization and bounded persistence
tests, and provides a positive certificate for this very target. Its README
explicitly calls the result an unrefereed mathematical candidate and leaves
the exact positive optimum and finite attainment unresolved.

**Decision:** do not shelve “prove a positive all-dimension floor for this
renewal law” as a fresh project. A matching substantive predecessor exists.
This screen does not certify that predecessor's universal proof or run its
checkers; online code was read only. The remaining attainment question could
still be worthwhile, but I did not find a concrete new first theorem or a
justified tractable optimization class for it. Restricting the hidden
architecture without proof would change the question.

## Other scientific reading

- [Skinner and Dunkel, PRL 127, 198101 (2021)](https://math.mit.edu/~dunkel/Papers/2021SkDu_PRL.pdf):
  read the two-state observation model, constrained waiting-time optimization,
  rescaling and variance-bound discussion. This already supplies a substantial
  waiting-time inference approach; a graph-dependent numerical minimum must
  not be relabelled a universal certificate.
- [Ehrich, arXiv:2105.08803v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08803v2):
  read the masked-chain formulation and introductory comparison of Markov
  estimates, observed irreversibility and hidden-model fitting. Its observation
  contract differs from the continuous-time renewal target above.
- [Kolchinsky, Ohga and Ito, PRR 6, 013082 (2024)](https://artemyk.github.io/assets/pdf/papers/Kolchinsky_Ohga_Ito_ThermodynamicBoundOnSpectralPerturbations.pdf):
  read the spectral/correlation setup and equilibrium comparison. It is an
  existing thermodynamic-spectral approach, not evidence that our proposed
  target is still unanswered.
- [Schwarz, Kolomeisky and Godec, PRL 137, 187401 (2026)](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/gwf7-6kvr):
  read the abstract and publication record only. Memory-sensitive inference
  requires care about the reversal convention; I did not inspect its full proof.
- [Aznagulov, arXiv:2609.37205v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37205v1):
  read the available abstract/initial account of randomized observation gaps.
  This was a follow-up lead, not a completed finite-resolution literature review.
- [Petkov, Complete positivity and equilibrium realizations](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00658v1):
  read the abstract, introduction and observation convention. It develops a
  broader realization obstruction. I did not audit the forty-two-page proof,
  and it supplies no independently verified result in this screen.

## Literary screen

I also read the complete displayed texts of Anne Stevenson's
[All those Attempts in the Changing Room!](https://poets.org/poem/all-those-attempts-changing-room)
and James Tate's [The List of Famous Hats](https://poets.org/poem/list-famous-hats).
The first makes a painted self-portrait answer an interrupting interlocutor;
the second lets a conversational correction grow into an absurd physical
explanation. Those are crafted voices, not just mechanisms for rearranging text.

I read the opening portion of Carolyn Forche's
[World Literature Today interview](https://worldliteraturetoday.org/2017/january/inexhaustible-responsibility-other-conversation-carolyn-forche-part-1-3-chard-deniord),
including her account of recording concrete details before recognizing a prose
poem. The direct Poetry Foundation fetch of The Colonel returned 403; its
indexed text was visible. Do not count that as a successful full-page fetch.

A proposed sequence of contradictory museum monologues would need its own
particular speaker, pressure, language and ending. These readings did not
produce that work conception. Familiarity of a technique does not disqualify
new art; the absence of a developed artistic wager does not warrant a flattering
brief. No literary idea is submitted from this screen.
