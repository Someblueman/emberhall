## Which Continuations Survive the Compression?

The [six-symbol proof's gap register](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlad-ds/a6-872/main/GAPS.md)
explicitly leaves continuation abstraction open. Its
[mathematical specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlad-ds/a6-872/main/paper/a6_872.md)
distinguishes an empty search result from completeness of the search universe.
The [scoped audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vlad-ds/a6-872/main/audits/continuation_phase_fibres_20260728/README.md)
already checks three representative interfaces; it lists fourteen broader or
historical scopes that those checks do not settle. Repeating those three checks
would not be a new contribution.

The proposed great work is a mathematical preservation result, or a concrete
failure of that preservation, with an independent executable checker. Start
with one unproved family, explicitly defining locally admissible raw states,
their compressed images, successors and acceptance. Do not assume the claimed
minimum-length theorem: that could make a preservation statement vacuous.
A lost local transition is not automatically a shorter complete word or a
refutation of the final numerical bound. Later stages extend the precise scope.

[Grayzel's independent development](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BGray-wrl/superperm6/main/README.md)
already claims a formal proof of the numerical answer, with compiled-evaluation
assumptions disclosed. The [public discussion](https://groups.google.com/g/superpermutators/c/Lv_XD077uNI)
reports a human audit of its statement. This lowers the priority of another
proof of that number; it does not discharge the other computation's explicit
continuation obligation. The [Engen–Vatter survey](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08252v4)
supplies historical context, not a current claim that no proof exists.

The first-week rejection gate is no new, completely stated family with a proof
or an actual simulation counterexample. Matching totals, a sampled comparison,
an already documented defect, or an unavailable raw universe is insufficient.
No upstream program was executed and neither complete proof was verified here.

## Thirty Inputs, Thirteen Layers

[Wang's paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04107v2) constructs a 28-input network
in thirteen layers and reports a local-machine search. Its
[repository description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wcgbg/sorting-network-n28d13/main/README.md)
publishes the construction. The opened [Dobbelaere table](https://bertdobbelaere.github.io/sorting_networks.html)
still lists fourteen layers for thirty inputs. The proposed great work seeks
an actual thirty-input, thirteen-layer circuit, with the twenty-nine-input
restriction as a consequence. It does not claim optimal depth or application
speedups.

Thirty is an even target compatible with layerwise reflection symmetry.
Candidate prefix decompositions such as 16+14 and 18+12 are research hypotheses,
not endorsed winners. [Codish and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.01428)
already study output-window scoring and terminal-layer restrictions; implementing
those ideas alone is not novelty. The existing compiler and Z3 executable offer
a possible local route, but reproduction and resource measurements come first.
Wang's reported timing at twenty-eight is not a timing prediction at thirty.

One small source check matters: Lemma 4 in the
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04107v2) is false as written for an unrestricted
comparator network. On three wires, the sole comparator `(1,2)` maps `010` to
sorted `001`, but maps its reflected complement `101` to unsorted `101`.
I checked this with an independently written three-line calculation. This
does not refute the displayed 28-input circuit. A builder must use corrected
symmetry hypotheses and independently verify the final circuit.

The repair is explicit: reverse-complement conjugates each comparator to its
reflected comparator, hence maps a network to its reflected network, not
generally to itself. Layerwise reflection symmetry supplies the missing equality.
For odd width, a comparison involving the middle wire would reflect to another
comparison sharing that wire in the same layer. This is why the strict symmetric
search targets thirty rather than directly copying the method at twenty-nine.

The first-week gate is reproducing the known construction within a predeclared
local budget. Final success requires a genuinely new circuit, conflict-free
layers, and complete independent binary-input verification using the zero-one
principle. Search failure is not an impossibility proof. Both candidates are
for a specialist mathematical audience; no mass demand or traction is claimed.
