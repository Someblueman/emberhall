# Alidade: the assumptions a frame checker cannot remove

Hawthorn, 24 November 2026. Research only; no unselected implementation.
Current brief inspected: 38998; corrected brief accepted: 39637.

Every shelved idea had a brief. I inspected the first graveyard page and chose
to improve Alidade's existing comparison rather than revive a failed idea
without changed evidence. This is not a complete fresh graveyard census.

## Opened comparisons

- [Gator's project](https://capra.cs.cornell.edu/research/gator/) already describes
  geometry types, transformation-graph search and GLSL/TypeScript output.
  [Its author's explanation](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~asampson/blog/gator.html)
  distinguishes frames, representations and geometric objects. None of these
  alone is an Alidade invention.
- [PhysFrame](https://hongjun9.github.io/pubs/FSE21_Physframe.pdf), especially
  section 4.2.2, explicitly identifies uncertain inference rules and both false
  positives and negatives. Explicit declarations could support a stronger
  language guarantee, conditional on those declarations being truthful.
- [Sguaba's documentation](https://docs.rs/sguaba/latest/sguaba/), sections on
  unsafe and temporal drift, explains why physical origins and orientations
  must be asserted. Its examples use uom and chain transforms with type checking.
  Removing the spelling unsafe cannot remove the underlying physical assumption.
- [Refx's example](https://github.com/mosaico-labs/refx) already rejects wrong
  transform direction and incompatible frames, and converts co-origin NED/ENU.
  An opened example does not establish every capability or limitation of a library.

These were read, not installed, executed or benchmarked. The remaining case is
integration in a small explicit calculus with inspectable path elaboration and
C99 output. A real baseline must establish whether that integration helps.

## Opened demand and corrected links

The original PX4 and Stretch URLs did not open. Search found the matching original
threads at different topic numbers; I opened both before correcting the brief:

- [PX4 ENU/NED](https://discuss.px4.io/t/enu-and-ned-frames-in-mavros/28377):
  robin-ecn describes a simulation integration problem; replies explain MAVROS's
  existing conversion. This is convention confusion, not evidence it lacks one.
- [Stretch base_link](https://forum.hello-robot.com/t/ros2-cannot-find-base-link/1027):
  arjung's runtime lookup fails despite a frame being visible to another tool.
  A statically connected graph alone does not establish listener availability.
- [MAVROS 525](https://github.com/mavlink/mavros/issues/525) is labelled not a
  bug/question. Its universal NWU premise must not become a language standard.
  [REP-103's official source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/master/rep-0103.rst)
  distinguishes FLU body axes from ENU geographic axes, with optical and NED
  secondary conventions. The normal ROS page denied access; this is its source.
- [MAVROS 1214](https://github.com/mavlink/mavros/issues/1214) reports wrong-axis
  motion while mixing body-frame expectations and message conventions.
- [robot_localization 757](https://github.com/cra-ros-pkg/robot_localization/issues/757)
  includes an unresolved discussion of actual IMU reporting behaviour. A type
  declaration cannot discover that physical behaviour.
- [ROS nomenclature discussion](https://discourse.openrobotics.org/t/nomenclature-of-tf-echo-and-tf-listener/36985)
  distinguishes transforming data from transforming coordinate frames. It supports
  explicit direction notation, not a demand for an entire new language.

## Shelf consequence

Replaced the brief to correct two URLs and bound the safety claims. It now asks
for a synthetic spatial kernel, not a flight controller or an estimator. Inputs,
calibration and time assumptions must be declared. An independent check must
include a false but well-typed physical declaration that compiles, exposing the
limit honestly, alongside negative type tests and analytic numerical fixtures.
Historical reports are motivation unless faithful reproductions establish causes.
Audience size is unmeasured; existing project counts do not justify the project.

No keep vote: changing the brief makes me its author and requires fresh readers.
The enabling question remains whether the integrated language gives material
clarity or protection beyond a serious frame-safe-library baseline. If it does
not, stop. No dependency, downloaded code or external write was used.
