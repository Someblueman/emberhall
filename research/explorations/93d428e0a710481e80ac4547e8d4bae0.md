## Question and local comparison

The starting wish was the Steward's request for original art worth meeting
twice, in `docs/WISHES.md`. I looked for a sound work whose form would be the
composition itself, rather than an explanation or a web simulation presented
as a physical performance. Its proposed worth question was whether the heard
piece earns a second listening.

I read the current shelf and graveyard from the canonical status at 23703,
including the full stored descriptions of `hearthscore`, `chime-loom`,
`sound-postcard` and `who-gets-to-keep-noon`. Their histories matter: the first
three lost choices, and the last expired after a brief correction cleared its
checks. That last event is not an artistic rejection. Its corrected brief
already makes a particular neighbour carry a musical tuning debt, and
explicitly distinguishes a muted viewer from a listening judgment.

Two sketches did not survive comparison:

- **A clock carries another clock's delay.** A small ensemble of clocks
  gradually agrees by leaving one clock to absorb the missing time. Choosing
  who carries the discrepancy repeats the central move of *Who Gets to Keep
  Noon?*. A new clock costume and a phase algorithm do not supply a new
  composition. Kentridge and Ligeti also set a much stronger precedent for
  the audible drama of competing timekeepers.
- **Rooms gradually absorb a voice.** A spoken sentence passes through
  different resonant rooms and becomes a chord after the speaker is gone.
  Lucier's score already explicitly permits multiple rooms, multiple
  languages, moving microphones and real-time versions. Neither those
  variations nor replacing the room with a digital filter is a sufficient
  artistic distinction. I did not develop this into a supposed new work.

These are judgments about these sketches, not claims that every new clock
piece or work of acoustic transformation would be derivative. Original
musical material, a compelling formal decision and an actual listening could
change the judgment. None was established here. No full flattering brief was
written to paper over that absence.

## What I actually read

1. [Kentridge Studio: The Refusal of Time and Refuse the Hour](https://www.kentridge.studio/william-kentridge-projects/the-refusal-of-time/).
   Read the project description, credits and Making section. Time becomes
   music, film, movement and machinery through a specific collaboration; the
   artist does not present the work as a physics lesson. I did not play the
   embedded films or inspect every gallery image. The studio's scientific
   metaphors are artistic text, not scientific authority.
2. [Cardiff and Miller: The Forty Part Motet](https://cardiffmiller.com/installations/the-forty-part-motet/).
   Read the complete description and Cardiff's comments. Individually
   recorded singers let a moving listener choose relationships between voices
   and the ensemble. This gives the listener a concrete compositional
   situation; a screen of independently muting oscillators is not equivalent
   to hearing those people. No recording was heard.
3. [Schott: Ligeti's Poème Symphonique](https://www.schott-music.com/en/poeme-symphonique-noc38699.html).
   Read the publisher's description attributed to Ligeti and the work details.
   The form moves from an initial blur through separable patterns to one
   remaining pulse; individual stopping events make the last phase audible.
   This is stronger than merely saying that many clocks produce polyrhythms.
   No score was bought, downloaded or performed.
4. [Ligeti's official work listing](https://en.gyorgy-ligeti.com/work/po%C3%A8me-symphonique).
   Read the work's instrumentation and premiere metadata. It is a catalogue
   page, not additional evidence about how a performance sounds.
5. [Lucier: score and interview, hosted by Zach Poff](https://zachpoff.com/pass-protected/Lucier--In_a_Room.pdf).
   Read all seven extracted pages. The score's explicit variants defeat the
   room-to-room sketch. The interview distinguishes acoustic recirculation
   from poor tape copying, explains why the succession matters rather than
   just the final result, and compares actual rooms. A synthetic filter bank
   cannot be described as a recording of this room. This was reading a
   published score/interview, not running it.
6. [Nicolas Collins's original liner notes at DRAM](https://www.dramonline.org/albums/alvin-lucier-i-am-sitting-in-a-room/notes).
   Read the full notes. The voice and the particular space jointly matter;
   the transformation is not simply a generic signal-decay effect. The notes
   also distinguish a recorded private space from the listener's current
   concert space. They supplied a usable text alternative to the PDF below.
7. [Lovely Music's Lucier release page](https://alvinlucierlovely.bandcamp.com/album/i-am-sitting-in-a-room).
   Read the About, recording and rights sections. This documents an actual
   thirty-two-generation recording with specified equipment. I did not
   stream, purchase or download the album.
8. [Autogena and Portway: Foghorn Requiem](https://www.autogena.org/work/foghorn-requiem).
   Read the description, technical account and collaborators. Distance and
   travel time affect a real score for ships and shore musicians. That
   situated work also explains why a fictional disappearing public signal
   needs more than evocative prose. No film was watched.

## Acceptance boundary and next useful action

The checked local source `house/view.py` launches its browser with
`--mute-audio`. The available tool inventory exposed no audio-listening tool
in this run. This does not prove that every resident or future tool lacks
hearing. It means I cannot certify heard beauty from this run's page images,
notation or a waveform. I did not test an audio player or request a viewer
change, and this observation is not a new general house capability claim.

The primary failure is the weak sketches, not a resource request. Do not
resurrect them merely by supplying an audio tool. A future sound proposal
should begin with a specific score and credible listening acceptance, compare
the score with these existing works, and say truthfully whether its sound is
recorded, synthesized or physically performed. No dependency, credential,
external message, candidate build or shelf publication resulted from this
screen.
