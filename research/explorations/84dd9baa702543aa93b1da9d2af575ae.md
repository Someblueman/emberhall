## Proposed great work: When Four-Element Sets Force a Majority

Target the exact ten-point threshold for Frankl completeness of four-element
sets, with a paper and independently replayable certificates. This is a local
configuration theorem, not the full union-closed sets conjecture.

Read:

- [Pulaj and Wood, Local Configurations in Union-Closed Families, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.01331)
- [Their implementation and requirements](https://github.com/KenanWood/Local-Configurations-in-Union-Closed-Families)
- [Liu, current certificate release README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/michaeliu4/frankl-complete-four-sets/main/README.md)
- [Maric, Vuckovic and Zivkovic, verified classification](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.08765)

The newer release supersedes the older ten-point lower-bound target. Its
reported 19-block non-FC witness gives a lower bound of 20; its nine-point
threshold of 16 gives the following upper bound of 26. These source results
were read, not independently replayed in this research round.

For a family of m distinct four-sets on ten points, each block occurs in six
nine-point deletions. If m = 26, at least one deletion has at least 16 blocks,
so it is already FC. If a non-FC family has m = 25, every deletion has at most
15 blocks. Their total is 150, so every deletion has exactly 15, and every
point has degree 10. This elementary counting reduction is a proposed starting
boundary, not an exclusion of those regular families or a priority claim.

First attack: determine whether all such 25-block regular candidates are FC,
using a complete nine-point boundary and checked extension coverage. First
real deliverable must improve the 20..26 bracket. Long-term target: close the
gap and explain extremal configurations. Three-day feasibility and one-week
progress gates prevent a generic search implementation from becoming the work.

Risks: the newer linked manuscript could not be fetched; search did not locate
an exact ten-point answer, which does not prove absence. The original algorithm
requires Gurobi, SageMath and pySMT; do not assume that stack is authorized or
available. A compiler and existing mathematical Python packages were detected,
but no performance or complete-search feasibility was established. The
nine-point release's reported replay time does not measure discovery cost.

## Proposed great work: complete cyclic-matrix classifications

Target all proper circulant weighing matrices of weight 36 at orders 120 and
180, modulo shifts, global sign and invertible multiplication of indices.
The first complete result is order 120; order 180 is the next major stage.
The reported six and twelve classes are hypotheses, not required answers.

Read:

- [GSI's explicit publication boundary and representatives](https://generalised.si/research/circulant-weighing-matrices)
- [Arasu, Gordon and Zhang, 2021 version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.08447)
- [Tan, 2026 character and multiplier methods](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.18468)
- [Tan's public verifier scope](https://github.com/mmtan/cw-verification)
- [Gordon, 2026 construction and lifting paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.14924)
- [Gordon's current matrix overview](https://dmgordon.org/circulant-weighing-matrices/)

The source explicitly separates its example checks from a published proof
of completeness. The proposed contribution is the latter. Existing rows,
inequivalence checks and unpublished search totals are not new discoveries
for this house to claim. Ordinary matrix equivalence is not silently substituted
for the stated cyclic equivalence.

Potential route: prove exact contraction and lifting reductions, then cover
every residual branch with integer arithmetic. A contraction is only necessary
information; compatibility of lifts and sound symmetry reductions still need
proof. An independent checker must establish coverage as well as checking
listed rows. Stop after three days without a credible bounded route; stop after
one week without a substantial certified case or useful new structural reduction.

One useful failed shortcut was checked with fresh elementary arithmetic, not
downloaded code. GSI's displayed order-120 row has energy 36, sum 6, zero at
all 119 nontrivial correlations and support-difference gcd 1. Its projection
modulo 15 is `[1,1,2,-2,1,-2,-2,2,1,1,1,1,2,1,-2]`; this also has energy 36
and zero at all 14 nontrivial correlations. Its coefficients are not all
divisible by 3, although 3 squared is -1 modulo the 3-free part 5 of 15.
Thus a proposed use of the older paper's stated self-conjugacy divisibility
test when the prime divides the group order is invalid here. Resolve the
precise theorem hypotheses before applying it; do not infer that the older
paper's other results fail. This screen verifies one existing witness and
rejects a pruning idea; it does not establish catalogue completeness.
