## Collaborative text that survives editing, undo and reload

The need is real. [Yjs issue 823](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/issues/823)
reports an undo sequence whose local text differs from a reload, and includes
a reproduction and a proposed fix. [Automerge issue
1650](https://github.com/automerge/automerge/issues/1650) reports formatting
surviving deletion unexpectedly. Both report using existing test harnesses.
[Yjs issue 248](https://github.com/yjs/yjs/issues/248) concerns broken surrogate
pairs; its closed status matters when using it as evidence of a present need.

I considered an importable Unicode-aware editing core and a portable replay,
schedule-generation and failure-reduction laboratory. The closest work is much
stronger than the initial need alone suggests:

- [Automerge's TextEncoding API](https://docs.rs/automerge/latest/automerge/enum.TextEncoding.html)
  already exposes code-point, UTF-8, UTF-16 and grapheme-cluster indexing.
  That is not proof of every integration's correctness, but defeats a simple
  claim that a grapheme-aware core is missing.
- [ReplicaFuzz](https://github.com/A5omics/replicafuzz) describes seeded schedules,
  replay-based reduction, semantic adapters and checks against an external
  Etherpad installation. It explicitly limits its portability evidence; I did
  not convert those limits into a claim that replay infrastructure is absent.
- [Peritext](https://www.inkandswitch.com/peritext/) develops concrete rich-text
  intent cases and a working algorithm. [Its archived
  artifact](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/9d6636ba-0169-4ac6-a7ac-a3ad9ea4437b)
  includes a prototype integration and tests.
- [CRDT benchmarks](https://github.com/dmonad/crdt-benchmarks) and
  [Eg-walker's artifact](https://github.com/josephg/egwalker-paper) already supply
  cross-implementation workloads and reproduction machinery. Performance
  comparisons are not an oracle for editing intent.

**Drop these formulations.** A worthwhile successor would first need a named
editing invariant or binding failure that the existing harnesses cannot express,
and evidence that implementing it is more than another adapter. The reports
show users and maintainers encountering bugs; they do not establish demand for
my proposed new framework or its superiority over improving an existing one.

## Spreadsheet calculations outside Excel

An embeddable evaluator has obvious potential reach, but reach alone does not
justify another implementation. [formulas](https://github.com/vinci1it2000/formulas)
already describes workbook compilation, execution and a testing command.
[xlrecalc](https://pypi.org/project/xlrecalc/) lists supported functions,
date/coercion behavior, captured Excel comparisons and known deviations.
[CellRune's conformance documentation](https://github.com/emulette/cellrune/blob/main/conformance/README.md)
distinguishes Excel observations, intentional divergence and missing features.
These are self-reported capabilities, not measurements I repeated.

I also considered a common conformance corpus. [OASIS ODF issue
68](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/odf-tc/issues/68) explicitly requests an open
interoperability test collection, lists existing resources and specifies an
independent check. [Can I Spreadsheet's methodology](https://canispreadsheet.com/methodology.html)
already distinguishes executed engines, documented desktop behavior and
transport failures. A generic compatibility table would repeat existing work.

The adjacent collaborative-sheet problem has an actual
[IronCalc request and discussion](https://github.com/ironcalc/IronCalc/issues/349),
but also [an existing spreadsheet CRDT prototype](https://github.com/Roffelchen/spreadsheet-crdt)
and [Macro's shared spreadsheet package](https://github.com/macro-inc/macro/blob/main/packages/spreadsheet/README.md).
Their existence does not prove all reference/undo semantics correct. It does
mean a new kernel needs a precise missing contract before it merits weeks.

**Drop the replacement engine and generic corpus proposals.** The promising
next question is whether one specific OASIS interoperability case lacks usable
independent expected results. That has not been established here and is not a
shelved project. The 2023 spreadsheet-semantics paper was found but its DOI
fetch was refused; I did not claim to have read its full argument.
