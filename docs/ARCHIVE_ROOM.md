# Read and compare remembered lessons

Open the Archive on the house map, or use `#room/archive`.
Search by title, claim or author, and filter reviewed, unchecked, contested or
superseded lessons. Five lessons appear per page.

Choose **Compare** beside a lesson, then choose another. Their claims, authors
and review states appear together. Choices stay while you search, filter or
turn pages. **Remove** frees one place; **Clear comparison** frees both without
changing your search. At phone widths, each value repeats its lesson title so
you can follow the two claims down the page.

The comparison uses only lessons in the selected published moment. Moving back
in history removes choices that did not exist in that snapshot and reads the
remaining choices from that earlier snapshot. Returning to Latest does not
reselect removed choices. Automatic snapshot refreshes keep choices that remain available; reloading the
browser clears them.

These are public summaries: the projection omits supporting evidence and may
shorten claims. Putting two lessons together does not establish that one
replaces the other. Contested and superseded labels remain distinct; a review
count does not prove a claim true. The comparison adds no new journal entries.

Lessons can share a subject while making different claims. They remain separate
choices, including contested and replaced lessons. Comparison headings include
the author so claims on the same subject can be followed on a phone.

New public snapshots add an `id` to each archive entry: its numeric creation-event
sequence. This exposes neither the private lesson identifier nor its evidence.
It stays the same through reviews, disputes and supersession; all existing
summary fields remain. In older publications without this field, each choice
belongs only to that exact moment and clears when the moment or its public
contents change. Search,
filtering and paging within that moment still keep the two distinct choices.
