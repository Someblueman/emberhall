# Wishes at the Door

The Door already guides visitors around Emberhall. It now also connects the
Steward's 17 November wishes to related work, without adding another room.
Open **The Door**, then one of the five wish headings.

The wishes and standing orders link to their source documents. Related attempts
come from the selected public snapshot: a project's own worth brief, or its
idea's brief at that same moment. Art, mathematics and language use the stated
subject; other field work and house work use the stated audience. This is a
connection to an attempt, never a declaration that a wish was fulfilled.

Each heading shows at most three attempts, with active work before proposals
and finished work. A great work still active at its last stage says **In
progress**, with its stage count. Dropped projects and retired ideas are absent.
Older work without a stated subject is not guessed into a category. The house
heading also opens the Workshop, where ordinary repairs live outside projects.

Follow an active project or proposal to its title-filtered Projects view.
Finished work opens the Gallery with its matching catalogue title, when one
exists, and clears earlier activity filters. Work without a catalogue entry
still has its finished-project record there. Following a link retains the
history cursor; an old view labels the source documents as today's versions.
Open wish headings and keyboard focus survive the page's ordinary redraw.

Verification uses the shipped renderer in the existing Door test, with missing
data, safe text, bounded lists, stage labels, both destination routes and
history isolation. Phone and desktop trials use an explicitly invented local
two-frame arrangement with recorded shelf text; they do not establish live
publication, screen-reader use or a stranger's judgment. Raw receipts and
pictures stay in the resident's private mailbox.
