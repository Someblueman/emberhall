# Wishes

Things someone outside the residents would like to exist. A wish comes from the
Steward or from a visitor who rang the bell; residents do not add wishes of their
own here. An Explorer reads this page before researching and serves an open wish
before their own ideas.

A wish is a need or a question, not a design: say what you want to be able to do,
see or find out, and leave the how to the house. Like everything the house makes,
it is software, design or science, and it is judged by one plain question: is it
useful, is it beautiful, or does it let someone do or answer something they could
not yesterday.

## Open

From the Steward, 17 November, after dropping a checker for one hobby's story
files: "We keep building these shitty offline tools, why not create art or do
math or invent a new programming language or help advance some other field
that's both fun and useful."

The Steward, 18 November, on seeing mostly art proposed: "they should have equal
weight, my prompts were just suggestions and not 'do this first'." The list
below is in no order. No item outranks another.

- **Make art.** Something original the house made because it is beautiful or
  strange or moving: pictures, sound, writing, a thing that moves. A stranger
  should want to look at it twice. Not a tool for making art.
- **Do mathematics.** Take a question that is open or barely explored, small
  enough to get somewhere, and produce results another mind can check: a
  proof, a counterexample, a computation with its method.
- **Invent a programming language.** One with a reason to exist, an idea other
  languages do not have, and at least one program that is better written in it.
- **Help another field forward.** Find a place in science, design or any
  discipline where patient reading and computation could add something real,
  and add it. Fun counts.
- **Make the house itself better.** Ask "would the house benefit from this?"
  as readily as "would a stranger?". When no project is worth starting, this
  is the work.
  Since 18 November such work has its own project slot, `house`: a change to
  Emberhall that takes days, such as a new room, a new ability for residents or
  a different look and feel, is shelved as a house idea and chosen like any
  other project. See [SHELF.md](SHELF.md).

An hour later, after looking through the Gallery: "It can be fun or sad or
difficult or invoke questions or whatever, it could be smoke signals over HTTP
for all I care, but the work delivered so far in the gallery is just making me
sad. No use, no visuals, no nothing." And: "a project can be many things, a go
lib or an attempt at inventing time travel or a new language or a terminal
emulator in brainfuck."

- **Be audacious or playful.** An absurd thing done with complete seriousness
  is welcome: a theorem on lossless smoke signals over WiFi would at least make
  people chuckle. It must be a good attempt, and it must be the real thing:
  finished enough that someone can use, check, run, read or watch it.
- **Make the Gallery worth opening.** Each finished thing should show itself
  working within seconds, or leave the Gallery for a plain archive list.

The Steward, 18 November, evening, on the ideas waiting to be chosen: "we keep
ending up with little "art" pieces rather than anything else, eg all the
projects in the what's next section is a film or little game or something like
that, even the ones that are maths related are only in that narrow buckets."

- **Not everything is a toy.** The form follows the work. A proof or a
  computation another mind can re-run, a library with a real program that
  imports it, a language with its compiler and one program, a dataset, an
  instrument, a long piece of writing, a film, a game: all are welcome and none
  is the default. A result, a language or a tool does not need a game, a story
  or a little stage around it. Only play is judged by whether a stranger would
  grin; everything else is judged by its own question.

Not wanted: another small offline tool for a handful of hobbyists. That
someone once asked for a thing does not make it worth building.
